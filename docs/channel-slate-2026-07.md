# The Channel Slate — July 2026 (v4)

Seventeen judged-membership channels — eight conversions and nine new **station identities** —
plus a consolidation pass of six retirements. Each station is a brief: the promise, what airs /
what never airs, canon from our own shelves, and the measured bench. Membership is a per-title
judgment (`Channel` tags), not a facet formula — the question asked of every title in the sweep
is *"which of these stations would program this?"*

Overlap between stations is by design — a film belongs everywhere it fits. The only rule is that
no two stations may collapse into the same identity.

**v4 changes from Eric's review of v3:** Anime Films RESTORED (anime movies must not be folded
into a series-round-robin channel); Kid-Friendly Shorts RESTORED and upgraded to a judged
conversion — its identity strengthens toward true short films (286 on the shelf: Pixar shorts,
golden-age cartoons, A Trip to the Moon, World of Tomorrow) over episodic minisode filler; The
Witching Hour rubric rewritten to be *Halloween-seasonal*, not merely good-horror (Silence of the
Lambs is a great film and a wrong answer); The Superstation brief now states the structural
guarantee against the representation worry; telemetry demoted from retirement-judge to
tie-breaker (the friend group is small enough that "unwatched" ≠ "worthless").

**v3 changes from Eric's review of v2:** four retirements VETOED and restored (Web Series and
Stunts & Pranks are not Public Access; Off the Beaten Path and Arthouse TV are strong destinations
— the v2 proposals came from pool-overlap math instead of channel identity, which was the wrong
lens). The washed [adult swim] / Adult Animation / Animation-for-Grown-Ups trio gets a real
identity split (below). The Witching Hour added — the adult twin of Spooky Season, completing the
seasonal symmetry Eric asked about (bench: 269). v2 changes retained: Liner Notes cut; The
Naughty List added.

## Conversions (existing channels, judged membership replaces the facet gate)

### 1. Kid-Friendly Films — `kid-films` · Kids & Family
**Promise:** the movie channel you can leave on for the kids and walk away. Bluey-tier trust.
**Airs:** gentle animation and live-action; nothing that needs a parent in the room.
**Never airs:** peril-forward adventure (Jurassic Park), menace (Gremlins), grief-forward films (Watership Down).
**Canon:** Toy Story · Land Before Time · Winnie the Pooh · Ratatouille · Mary Poppins.
**Bench:** ~700 today by facet; judgment will trim the mislabeled edge.

### 2. Family Movie Night — `family-night` · Kids & Family
**Promise:** everyone on the couch, including the grown-ups. Reaches to Jurassic-Park-tier PG-13.
**Airs:** the family adventure canon — Star Wars, E.T., Back to the Future, Raiders, Ghostbusters, Moana, Spider-Verse.
**Never airs:** raunch comedies riding an "Adventure" genre tag (Tommy Boy), adult war/spy drama (Dirty Dozen, Casino Royale), scary-first films.
**Canon:** in ⇒ Jurassic Park, Star Wars IV, E.T., The Goonies, Toy Story · out ⇒ Anchorman, Lawrence of Arabia, Poltergeist.
**Bench:** ~550 by the interim gate; judged target ~450–600.

### 3. The Family Room — `family-tv` · Kids & Family
**Promise:** kid-safe shows all afternoon, every show a safe stop.
**Airs:** Bluey → Avatar → DuckTales → Sesame Street, round-robin.
**Never airs:** TV-PG-but-adult shows (Doctor Who is fine; Duckman is not — judgment, not rating).
**Bench:** 122 series / ~9,000 episodes by the interim gate.

### 4. Holiday Cheer — `holiday-cheer` · Seasonal (STRICT)
**Promise:** actually-Christmas movies. The Rudolf/Frosty rule.
**Airs:** films that are *about* the holiday — Grinch, It's a Wonderful Life, Muppet Christmas Carol, Home Alone.
**Never airs:** films merely *set at* Christmas (Gremlins — currently airing here; the bug that started all this).
**Bench:** ~74 today, honest membership probably ~60 — small and correct beats big and wrong.

### 5. Spooky Season — `spooky-season` · Seasonal (BROAD)
**Promise:** the October feeling. A seasonal *mood*, deliberately generous — Gremlins in October is right.
**Airs:** monsters, witches, haunted houses, Halloween specials, kid-safe through grown-up.
**Never airs:** nothing on strictness grounds; membership is "does it feel like October."
**Bench:** ~300.

### 6. [adult swim] — `adult-swim` · Animation Hall of Fame *(conversion — identity restore)*
**Promise:** the actual network. If it aired in the Williams Street block, it lives here.
**The wash today:** Superjail and Frisky Dingo — real adult swim broadcasts — sit in Adult
Animation's path list, while [adult swim] carries Harley Quinn (HBO Max). Judged membership
restores the literal broadcast identity: Aqua Teen, Sealab, Metalocalypse, Venture Bros,
Superjail, Frisky Dingo, Off the Air, Space Dandy, Rick and Morty.
**Never airs:** adult animation that never touched the block — that's the next channel's job.

### 7. Adult Animation — `adult-animation` · Animation Hall of Fame *(conversion — absorbs Animation for Grown-Ups)*
**Promise:** animation as an adult art form — Movies + TV, from MTV's Liquid Television lineage
to Bakshi to the anime masterworks.
**Airs:** Æon Flux, The Maxx, Love Death & Robots, Death Parade, Akira, Heavy Metal, Fritz the
Cat, End of Evangelion, Waltz with Bashir, MFKZ — plus Harley Quinn and the other non-block
adult toons that were diluting [adult swim].
**Never airs:** adult swim broadcasts (channel 6 owns the block), kids' animation of any kind.
**Bench (measured):** 43 hard-rated animated films + the TV side; the merged pool comfortably
clears 150. Animation for Grown-Ups (a 481-title intensity-gate approximation of this exact
identity) retires into it.

### 8. Kid-Friendly Shorts — `kid-shorts` · Kids & Family *(conversion — identity strengthened)*
**Promise:** a real shorts program — the theatrical and festival tradition, not chopped-up TV.
**Airs:** Pixar shorts (Luxo Jr., Bao, One Man Band), golden-age theatrical cartoons (What's
Opera, Doc?), early cinema (A Trip to the Moon), Wallace & Gromit shorts, Hertzfeldt's World of
Tomorrow, the Roald Dahl shorts.
**Never airs:** episodic minisodes as filler — a Bluey minisode is television and lives in The
Family Room; this channel's identity is the SHORT FILM. (Per Eric: "we HAVE many good shorts
that are not TV shows.")
**Bench (measured):** 286 movies at ≤40 min before judging; the kid-safe core is strong.

## New stations

### 9. The Monster Club — `big-kid-horror` · Kids & Family
**Promise:** the kids-allowed horror clubhouse. Scary the way a sleepover is scary.
**Airs:** Gremlins, The Monster Squad, Hocus Pocus, Coraline, ParaNorman, Beetlejuice, Tremors, the Universal/Hammer monsters, Goosebumps-tier.
**Never airs:** dread-horror (Ju-on, The Ring), gore, anything that follows a kid to bed. Poltergeist sits exactly on the line — judged out.
**Canon:** in ⇒ Gremlins, Monster Squad, Hocus Pocus, Coraline · out ⇒ The Exorcist, Ju-on, Saw.
**Bench probe:** ~100–140. Born from Eric's ask: "I wouldn't want them to randomly come on after a cozy flick."

### 10. The Superstation — `superstation` · The Networks
**Promise:** the movie you stop on while flipping, any Saturday afternoon of your life. You've
seen it; you'll watch it again anyway.
**Airs:** the '75–'10s crowd-approved rewatchables across comedy/action/thriller — The Fugitive,
Point Break, The Rock, Groundhog Day, My Cousin Vinny, Beverly Hills Cop, The Matrix, Home Alone.
**Never airs:** homework cinema, anything you must start from minute one to enjoy, prestige misery.
**Bench (measured):** 422 films at rewatch≥70/energy≥55 in 1980–2009 alone. This channel builds itself.
**The representation guarantee:** channel pools are computed independently — Superstation
membership never removes a film from anywhere. The Matrix stays on Sci-Fi & Fantasy, The '90s
and Keanu by mechanical filters no sweep touches, and the sweep judges every station's question
independently per title (never "where does this best fit"). Canon entries can pin
cross-representation permanently.

### 11. After Dark — `after-dark` · The Networks
**Promise:** the kids are asleep. R-rated comfort — hangout comedies, action classics, crime that's fun.
**Airs:** Animal House, The Breakfast Club, Lethal Weapon, The Usual Suspects, Tropic Thunder, Stand by Me, Fast Times.
**Never airs:** kids' content, misery-prestige, horror (Spooky Season and Gorehound own that).
**Bench (measured):** 335 films at R / imdb≥7 / rewatch≥65.

### 12. Public Access — `public-access` · The Networks
**Promise:** 1 a.m. UHF static — found footage, VHS dumpster, anti-comedy, outsider video.
**Airs:** Found Footage Festival (77 on disk), Everything Is Terrible! (50 on disk), industrial/training-video ephemera, psychotronic shorts, public-access surrealism.
**Never airs:** anything with a marketing department.
**Bench:** 127+ on-disk before judging the wider misc/shorts shelf. The most *this-library* channel on the slate.

### 13. Whodunit Weekend — `whodunit` · The Networks
**Promise:** rainy-Sunday armchair sleuthing. A body in the library, never a body in the walls.
**Airs:** Knives Out, Murder on the Orient Express, Death on the Nile, Clue, The Thin Man, Brick, Columbo-tier TV movies.
**Never airs:** serial-killer bleakness (Se7en), slashers wearing a mystery hat.
**Bench probe:** Mystery-genre-adjacent pool is 479; the true cozy core is likely 120–180.

### 14. Lazy Sunday — `lazy-sunday` · The Networks
**Promise:** the nap-adjacent afternoon epic. Long, sweeping, half-watched from the couch — war
films, seafaring, space programs, escapes.
**Airs:** The Great Escape, Bridge on the River Kwai, The Hunt for Red October, Apollo 13, Lawrence of Arabia, Master and Commander.
**Never airs:** anything frantic; anything the kids would interrupt.
**Note:** this is the channel that *receives* the adult-adventure films Family Movie Night must exclude — every exclusion needs a home.

### 15. Date Night — `date-night` · The Networks
**Promise:** couples' evening, charm-forward. Rom-coms and romances that end well or at least end warmly.
**Airs:** When Harry Met Sally-tier through Moonstruck, Sense & Sensibility, 10 Things I Hate About You.
**Never airs:** weepies (Bring Tissues owns those), period misery, break-up dramas.
**Bench probe:** Romance genre is 1,066; the curated core likely 150–250.

### 16. The Naughty List — `naughty-list` · Seasonal, Dec 1 – Jan 2
**Promise:** Christmas after bedtime. Tinsel, whiskey, and violence.
**Airs:** Violent Night, Bad Santa, Die Hard 1–2, Black Christmas ('74), Better Watch Out,
Lethal Weapon, The Ref, Krampus, Gremlins 2, Scrooged, Christmas Vacation, Batman Returns.
**Never airs:** anything on Holiday Cheer's strict list — the two are the same evening, different hours.
**Bench (measured):** ~25 adult-rated Christmas-adjacent + ~9 PG-13 spice; honest judged pool
~25–30, which a five-week seasonal window wears comfortably. Resolves the Die Hard debate by
giving each answer its own channel.

### 17. The Witching Hour — `witching-hour` · Seasonal, Oct 1 – Nov 1
**Promise:** October after the kids are asleep — the adult twin of Spooky Season, exactly as
The Naughty List is Holiday Cheer's.
**The rubric is SEASONAL ATMOSPHERE, not horror quality:** "does this feel like Halloween —
autumn, witches, haunted houses, the ritual of the season?" The Silence of the Lambs is a
masterpiece and a wrong answer; Suspiria is a right one.
**Airs:** Halloween, Trick 'r Treat, Suspiria, The Craft, Sleepy Hollow, Evil Dead, The Fog,
The Witch, Rosemary's Baby, Pumpkinhead, Halloween III.
**Never airs:** kid-safe monster fare (The Monster Club owns it), gore-for-gore (Gorehound's),
great horror-adjacent thrillers that don't smell of October.
**Bench:** 269 by the loose probe; the honestly-Halloweeny judged core ~120–150.

The four seasonal channels now form a clean 2×2: family × adult, October × December.

*(Liner Notes was cut in v2 — the Music-genre pool is noisy and the honest core is too thin; its
concert docs stay reachable via Musicals & Music and Documentaries. Not folded into Public
Access, per Eric.)*

## The consolidation pass — 6 retirements (v4: Anime Films and Kid-Friendly Shorts also restored)

A retirement is only safe when a stronger identity now owns the content. Retiring a catalog
channel just disables it (content stays reachable everywhere else).

**Absorbed by new stations:**
1. **Summer Blockbusters** (seasonal) → The Superstation *is* this identity, year-round.
2. **Sweetheart Cinema** (Feb window) → Date Night owns romance-that-ends-warmly all year, February included.

**Merged into an existing near-twin:**
3. **So Bad It's Good** (10) → Schlock Theater. Same joke, thinner pool.
4. **Silent Comedy** (18) → Silent & Pre-Code. Keaton and Chaplin already live there.
5. **Sing-Along Musicals** (902) → identity split between Musicals & Music and the family cluster; retire.
6. **Animation for Grown-Ups** (481) → the rebuilt Adult Animation (its intensity gate was an
   approximation of that identity all along).

**Restored across v3/v4 (wrongly proposed earlier):** Web Series, Stunts & Pranks, Off the
Beaten Path, Arthouse TV, Anime Films (anime *movies* must not fold into a series round-robin),
Kid-Friendly Shorts (upgraded to a judged conversion instead) — all keep their channels.

**Optional deeper cuts** (say the word): That '80s Horror + '80s Sci-Fi (decade×genre
intersections), and folding Korean (30) / Russian (46) / Scandinavian (74) into World Cinema.
Worth ~5 more if the guide still feels crowded.

**Net effect: 159 − 6 + 9 = 162.** Count crept up as vetoes restored good channels — identity
quality is winning over guide size, which reads as the right trade. The optional cuts remain
available; telemetry is a tie-breaker at this friend-group scale, never a retirement judge
(most channels will show unwatched most weeks and still be worth having).
