# Batman '66 (S1886) - 5 collected editions, 109 issue files. DC, Zone-Empire trade rips.
#
# ONE run, ONE coordinate, and it is not the obvious one. Batman '66 was published twice over: as 30
# printed magazines AND as 73 online DIGITAL CHAPTERS. Every issue file on this shelf is a chapter -
# item 61643's title page reads "THE RIDDLER'S RUSE PART 1" and item 61642's cover carries a large 73 -
# and the folder that holds them runs 001 to 073, which is the chapter count and not the print count.
# So a range written in PRINT issue numbers would be wrong by a factor of ~2.4 on this shelf.
#
# Each trade's copyright page states BOTH numberings, which is what makes the choice provable rather
# than inferred: "as BATMAN '66 1-5 and online as BATMAN '66 Chapters 1-15". The chapter half is the
# one recorded here.
#
# Vol. 03 is the one book whose copyright block I could not find (p2, p3, p4, p5, p6 and the last page
# are all art or contents). It is written as the COMPLEMENT between two ranges I did read - Vol. 02
# ends at chapter 30, Vol. 04 begins at chapter 46 - and the page arithmetic agrees: 169pp against
# Vol. 01's 175pp and Vol. 02's 178pp, all three holding fifteen chapters. It carries a lower
# confidence and says so.
#
# The five ranges tile 1-73 exactly with no gap and no overlap.
#
# Two folders hold the same chapters twice: the "Digital First" rips are guided-view (one panel per
# page, so chapter 1 is 96pp) and the "Static Image Version" rips are ordinary pages (22pp). That is a
# duplicate RIP of one run, not two runs, and both fall inside the same ranges.
