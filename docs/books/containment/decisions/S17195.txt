#   Vol. 04 (p3) SUPERMAN: WORLD OF NEW KRYPTON 6-12

# Three different numbering spaces across four books, none of them this shelf. And the three issue files
# on the shelf are a FOURTH thing: Superman - Last Stand of New Krypton #1, #2, #3, a separate 2010

#
# That is what makes a range here actively dangerous rather than merely wrong. Vol. 03 legitimately
# collects WORLD OF NEW KRYPTON #1-5; written as #1-5 on this shelf it would call all three Last Stand

# The shelf needs SPLITTING, not ranging: the New Krypton trade line, World of New Krypton, and Last
# Stand of New Krypton are three different things sharing one Series row.
