# Black Hammer (S64298) - 28 collected editions, 10 issue files. EVERY range on this shelf is retracted.
#
# This shelf is 28 books and 10 loose files, and NOT ONE of the loose files belongs to a run any of the
# books collect. Dark Horse published Black Hammer almost entirely in trade: the library holds the whole
# programme as trades, Library Editions and Omnibuses, and the only single issues here are from three
# unrelated satellites -
#
#   Black Hammer - Colonel Weird & Little Andromeda Shorts   #1-4 (2022 Substack shorts, 2-33pp)
#   Black Hammer - Spiral City                               #1-3 (2024 mini)
#   plus four unnumbered one-shots and shorts (Encyclopedia, Madame Dragonfly, Visions-Robertson)
#
# Every one of those numbers from #1. So a range like Secret Origins' #1-6 - which is a TRUE statement
# about Black Hammer (2016) #1-6 - can only ever match a file from a different series, and that is exactly
# what it was doing. All fifteen assignments on this shelf were wrong:
#
#   Secret Origins (#1-6)   held Substack Shorts #1-3 AND Spiral City #1-3
#   The Event (#7-13)       held the Encyclopedia, the Madame Dragonfly short and the Visions-Robertson short
#   Visions v02 (#4-12)     held Substack Short #4
#   World of Black Hammer Omnibus Book 01 (LOCG #1-13) held five other TRADES as a container-in-container
#
# So the ranges are retracted not because they are false but because there is nothing here they can be
# true ABOUT. If the main run's floppies are ever added, Secret Origins' #1-6 and The Event's #7-13 are
# worth re-reading off the books - they are the plausible ones. Visions is not: GCD gives v01 #1-5 and
# v02 #4-12, which OVERLAP, and the Visions series ran eight issues, so neither was read off the book.
#
# Four books were flagged as single issues and are corrected here by reading their titles: Black Hammer
# '45 (120pp, collects the four-issue '45 mini - and its IssueNo was 45, taken from the "'45" of the
# TITLE), Hammer of Justice! (160pp, the five-issue Justice League crossover), The Last Days of Black
# Hammer (131pp, the Substack serial) and Streets of Spiral (123pp, the shorts anthology).
#
# The shelf is CONFLATED - the main run, Visions, Spiral City, the '45 mini and the Substack shorts all
# sit here and each numbers from #1 - and is deliberately left that way: with every range retracted
# nothing can nest, so the conflation costs nothing today.
