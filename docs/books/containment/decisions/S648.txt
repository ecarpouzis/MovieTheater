# Aliens (S648) - 6 collected editions, 10 issue files. One range stored, five refused.
#
# The ten issues here are two separate Dark Horse miniseries, each numbering from #1:
#   Aliens - Book One #1-6   (1988) - published as ALIENS: OUTBREAK
#   Aliens - Book Two #1-4   (1989) - published as ALIENS: NIGHTMARE ASYLUM
#
# Aliens Omnibus Volume 1 states its contents on p2 and p3 and it holds BOTH of them: the contents page
# lists OUTBREAK, NIGHTMARE ASYLUM, FEMALE WAR, THEORY OF ALIEN PROPAGATION and THE ALIEN, and the
# copyright block says "This volume collects material previously published as the Dark Horse graphic
# novels Aliens: Outbreak; Aliens: Nightmare Asylum; and Aliens: Female War; and stories from the Dark
# Horse comic book Dark Horse Presents: Aliens Platinum Edition."
#
# That is a list of TITLES, not a range - the recurring shape of an omnibus - and the two titles we hold
# both restart at #1. On this shelf the two numberings overlap, so the single range #1-6 covers Book One
# #1-6 and Book Two #1-4 together, and every one of the ten files it takes IS in this book. The overlap
# that usually makes a range unsafe is harmless here for the specific reason that the omnibus contains
# both of the colliding runs. It is stored at 0.90 rather than higher because it is a range standing in
# for a list, and because this is the publisher's Aliens catch-all folder: if another Aliens mini
# numbering from #1 is ever filed here, this range must be re-examined before it is trusted.
#
# LOCG's #1-24 covers the same ten files by being a hull four times too wide. It is refused in favour of
# the read one.
#
# Omnibus v02 through v06 collect later Dark Horse Aliens series - none of whose issues are in this
# library - and no provider offers a range for any of them.
