# In these Son of Ultron rips the copyright page is archive index 2, and the statement sits at the TOP of
# it, not the bottom — the band a DC trade normally uses is art here. All six were read.

#   Vol. 06 Weirder Things           "TEEN TITANS GO! 31-36 and online as ... Digital Chapters 61-72"
#                                                                                        -> 31-36
#
# Every book names TWO coordinates — the print issue and the digital chapter, six issues against twelve
# chapters each time. The ranges are in PRINT ISSUE numbers, which is the coordinate the shelf's files
# are numbered in. Taking the chapter numbers instead would double every range and make Vol. 02 claim
# what Vol. 01 holds. The ladder tiles 1-36 with no gap; files #37-71 are outside every trade.
#
# WHY THE ARITHMETIC LOOKS WRONG AND IS NOT (§8 step 5): files 001-012 are 93-124 "pages" each and files
# 013-071 are 23-26. The first twelve are guided-view rips with ONE PANEL PER PAGE — file 001 opens on a
# single Cyborg panel from "Food Fright", the same story that runs as full pages at index 4-6 of Vol. 01.
# Their panel counts sum to about 1,306 over the 250-odd story pages of print #1-12, which is 5.2 panels
# per page: right for this art. Read as digital chapters instead they would be 10.4 panels per page,
# which this book never does. So they ARE print issues #1-12, and a page-arithmetic guard comparing a
# 131pp trade against six 106-"page" files will refuse a nesting that is in fact correct.
