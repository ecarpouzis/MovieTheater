# B.P.R.D. (S1715) — 25 collected editions, 1 issue file.
#
# B.P.R.D. is a chain of separately-numbered miniseries — Hollow Earth, The Soul of Venice, Plague of
# Frogs, The Dead, The Black Flame, 1946, 1947 and the rest — and every one of them starts at #1. That is
# why almost every claim on this shelf is #1-5 or #1-6: the same range, on twenty different books, in
# twenty different minis' numbering. The shelf holds one issue file (#1946), so nothing is at risk today,
# but the spans are indistinguishable from each other and none can be written as this Series' range.
#
# Two of them are the case already recorded in PLAN.md §11: Omnibus Book 08 (14938) and Book 10 (14940)
# went from honest silence to a LOCG #1-5. They are 407pp and 420pp books; #1-5 is one mini's worth.
