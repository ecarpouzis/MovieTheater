# Real Girl (S14423) - the Kodansha run, v01-v12. The manga sweep read the archives' chapter tokens and
# refused the shelf because v03 (c9-12) overlapped v04 (c12-15). The BOOKS settle it against the rip.
#
# Eleven volumes print a CONTENTS panel at p002 numbering each chapter "episode*NN" with its start page,
# and all eleven were read. v01's p002 is art; its archive names c1-c4 on its own pages and v02's contents
# opens at episode 5, so v01 is c1-c4.
#
# THE OVERLAP IS A RIPPER ARTEFACT. v03's contents lists episode 9, 10 and 11 and then a "Special Chapter:
# Spring fever" at p119 - the pages the archive labels c012 inside v03 ARE that special, misnamed. v04's
# contents opens at episode 12. So c12 belongs to v04 alone, and the ladder tiles c1-c46 over all 12
# volumes with no gap and no overlap. (The same danke-Empire habit - numbering an unnumbered extra with
# the NEXT chapter's number - is what produced the identical false overlaps in Vinland Saga and Grand Blue
# Dreaming.)
#
# Unnumbered extras are not claimed: v03's "Special Chapter", v06's "Special ver." and v12's closing
# "final episode" carry no episode number, so each range stops at the last numbered episode.
