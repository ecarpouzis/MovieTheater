# The library holds NO Bone single issues — not one. The whole Bone (1991) tree is nine colour volumes,
# the 1,319pp one-volume Complete Cartoon Epic, and a scatter of specials in \Misc (Holiday Special,
# Sourcebook, Handbook, the 10th Anniversary Special, Rose, Tall Tales, More Tall Tales), plus
# Stupid, Stupid Rat-Tails and the Mini Comics in their own folders. So nothing here can be de-duplicated
# against a floppy, whatever these ranges say; what they have to be is RIGHT.
#
# THE SEAM. The nine curated ranges are:
#
#     1-6   7-11   12-19   [21-27]   28-32   33-37   38-43   44-49   50-55
#
# Every volume abuts its neighbour exactly — 6|7, 11|12, 27|28, 32|33, 37|38, 43|44, 49|50 — with one
# exception: Vol. 03 ends at 19 and Vol. 04 starts at 21. Issue #20 falls into a hole the ladder has
# nowhere else, and Bone ran 55 issues with no gap. The ladder does not tile, and #20-27 is the only
# reading that makes it tile.
#
# THE ARITHMETIC AGREES. Pages per issue across the shelf sit at 22-26:
#     V1 143/6=24  V2 122/5=24  V3 174/8=22  V5 117/5=23  V6 119/5=24  V7 153/6=26  V8 137/6=23
#   Vol. 04 at #20-27 is 175/8 = 22 — exactly its neighbour Vol. 03. At #21-27 it is 175/7 = 25, the
#   highest figure on the shelf and an outlier against the volume it sits next to.
#
# GCD says 21-27 and GCD is the reason the row is wrong. Per §7 it indexes STORIES, not books, so its
# silence about #20 is not a denial — and here its claim is simply short by one.
