# ONE RUN of ongoing issues, but the shelf carries a real numbering fault underneath it. All fourteen
# "issue" files are 1pp variant COVER scans (#2 five times, #3, #5, #6 twice, #7 twice, #8) - and TWO of
# them are `Thor Annual 01 (2015)` covers stored with IssueNo = 1. The annual is not issue #1 of the
# ongoing; it is a separate book in its own coordinate, and while it is numbered 1 on this shelf a range of
# #1-5 covers it by accident. That is flagged rather than worked around: I have not changed any number.
#
# Both ranges read off the back cover (p-2 in these BroadCast-Empire rips). Vol. 02 also reprints THOR
# ANNUAL #1 and WHAT IF? (1977) #10; the stored range is only the Thor (2014) part, which is what its own
# back cover states as a range.
