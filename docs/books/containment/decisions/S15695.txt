# Dark Horse published this as a chain of separately-numbered five-issue miniseries. Our files carry a
# CONTINUOUS library index (000-025) and each arc own count in the same name
# (Witchfinder 016 - City of the Dead 01 of 05). Every volume is titled with its arc, so the volumes map
# to the files by NAME, with no number needed from any provider:
#
#   Vol. 01 In the Service of Angels  -> 001-005     Vol. 04 City of the Dead     -> 016-020
#   Vol. 02 Lost and Gone Forever     -> 006-010     Vol. 05 The Gates of Heaven  -> 021-025
#   Vol. 03 The Mysteries of Unland   -> 011-015
#
# The arithmetic confirms every one. Each trade carries 11-33pp of front and back matter over the sum of
# its issues pages, and each omnibus is the sum of three trades:
#   V1 120pp of story in a 153pp book   V4 124 in 135     Omni 01: 153+134+155 = 442 vs 438pp
#   V2 116 in 134   V3 129 in 155       V5 123 in 141     Omni 02: 135+141+144 = 420 vs 425pp
#
# GCD says #1-5 for each of the four volumes it knows — the ARC numbering, five issues each. It agrees
# with the title mapping on COUNT and disagrees on COORDINATE, and that is exactly why this series had
# resolved nothing: on a shelf whose files are numbered continuously, four editions all claiming #1-5 is
# a collision, not a containment.
#
# Omnibus 02 is the one that does not close. 425pp is City of the Dead + The Gates of Heaven + The Reign
# of Darkness, but Reign of Darkness lives in Series 21753 and is not numbered here at all, so the range
# recorded covers only the part of its contents this Series can name.
