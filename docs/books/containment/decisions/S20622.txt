# Ultimate Spider-Man (S20622) — 17 collected editions, 10 single issues held.
#
# THE SHELF: Marvel\_Ultimate Comics\Ultimate Spider-Man (2000)\01 Ultimate Spider-Man (2000) holds the
# seventeen trades. The ten single issues are not there at all — they sit in two reading-order trees,
# _Marvel Major Event Chronology\2011.02 - Death of Spider-man (issues 153-160) and \2009.02 - Ultimatum
# (issues 132, 133).
#
# THE EVIDENCE. Marvel prints the collected issues on the BACK COVER (PLAN.md §4.2 says the last page for
# this publisher, and that is exactly where it is). Every one of the seventeen was opened and read. The
# quotes below are those back covers verbatim.
#
# THE LADDER TILES from #40 to #133 with no gap and no overlap:
#   40-45  46  47-53  54-59  60-65  66-71  72-78  79-85  86-90  91-96  97-105  106-111  112-117
#   118-122  123-128  129-133
# Vol. 04 (#22-27) sits before it; Vols 01-03, 05 and 06 are not in the library, which is why the run
# starts where it does rather than at #1.
#
# WHAT WAS THERE BEFORE, AND WHY IT MATTERED. Every provider claim on this shelf was wrong.
#   LOCG gave Vol. 07 #7-7, Vol. 08 #8-8, Vol. 10 #10-10, Vol. 12 #12-12 ... — the volume ORDINAL echoed
#   back as a range (§6.2). ComicVine was misaligned by title matching: Vol. 18 and Vol. 20 BOTH claimed
#   #106-111, Vol. 12 claimed #54-59 (that is Vol. 10), Vol. 22 claimed #118-122 (that is Vol. 20).
#   Worst of all, LOCG put #132-133 on Vol. 11 CARNAGE — whose back cover says #60-65 — and 132 and 133
#   are two of the ten issues this library actually holds. That claim would have called both of them
#   redundant with a book that does not contain them.
#
# Vol. 09 is a genuine single-issue claim, not an artefact: its back cover says "Ultimate Spider-Man #46
# and Ultimate Six #1-7", and 46 is not the volume ordinal 9, so §6.2's discriminator keeps it. The
# Ultimate Six miniseries is a different Series and is not named by this range.
