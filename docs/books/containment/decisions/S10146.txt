# Justice League Unlimited (S10146) - 3 collected editions, 54 issue files. Every range refused,
# and the reason is the SHAPE of the books, not a gap in the evidence.
#
# At first sight this is the one shelf where the books and the issues belong together: three 2020 DC
# digests of the animated-series comics, and we hold Justice League Unlimited (2004) #1-40 nearly
# complete. Reading them settles it the other way. Their copyright pages say what they are:
#
#   Time After Time  "Originally published in single magazine form in Adventures in the DC Universe [...],
#                     League Adventures 28, 30, 34, Justice League Unlimited 9, 19."
#   Galactic Justice "Originally published in single magazine form in Justice League Unlimited [...] 46."
#
# These are THEMATIC ANTHOLOGIES. Six chapters apiece, drawn from three different comics - Adventures in
# the DC Universe, Justice League Adventures and Justice League Unlimited - and scattered inside each.
# Time After Time holds JLU #9 and #19 and nothing between them. There is no contiguous range that
# describes any of these books, so the right answer is not a better range, it is none: storing #9-19 to
# capture two issues would take nine that the book does not contain.
#
# That also explains the providers. LOCG's #9-34 (Time After Time) and #11-40 (Hocus Pocus) are the HULLS
# of scattered selections - twenty-six and thirty issues claimed by 158pp and 143pp digests - which is
# what a hull looks like when a data source flattens a list into a span. Between them the three were
# nesting twenty-six files, each claiming issues at least one of the others also claimed.
#
# Two of the three were flagged as single issues and are corrected here by reading: 143pp and 158pp
# digests. While that flag stood they could not even be decided.
#
# The shelf also holds the 2025 JLU relaunch - seven variant covers of its #3 - which all carry a span of
# #4-6, and one 2007 issue carrying #0-0. Noise on issue files; flagged, not used.
