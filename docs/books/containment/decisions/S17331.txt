# Superman: Up in the Sky (S17331) - 1 collected edition, 6 issue files. Refused.
#
# An unusual and instructive one. Up in the Sky was not published as its own comic - it ran as a serial
# inside SUPERMAN GIANT, the Walmart anthology, and the six issue files here are Superman Giant #1-6.
# LOCG's #3-16 is therefore in the right coordinate system and probably right about the book: the serial
# ran in Giant #3-16, and the range covers four of our six files.
#
# It is refused because those files are ANTHOLOGY issues. Each Superman Giant holds several serials, only
# one of which is in this trade, and nesting a whole anthology issue inside one story's collection files
# it under a book that holds a fraction of it. Under-claiming costs shelf space; this would cost the other
# serials their place.
