# X-Force v1 (1991) (S94741) - 5 collected editions, 49 issue files. Marvel.
#
# One run, one numbering: the loose files are X-FORCE #43 through #123 and every trade that states a
# range states it in the same numbers.
#
# Three of the five state their contents and are written. All three land ON files we hold, which is why
# this shelf was worth doing: Vol. 07 collects #49-56 and we hold #50-56; Milligan and Allred Vol. 02
# collects #121-129 and we hold #123. Those are real nestings that the shelf could not make before.
#
# Two are refused. X-Force Vol. 01 - Force To Be Reckoned With and Vol. 02 - Under The Gun are Marvel
# Premiere Edition rips with no back-cover COLLECTING line: their last page is a Marvel house ad and the
# two pages before it are story. What I could read is where each one STARTS - Vol. 01 opens on a
# reproduced cover of THE NEW MUTANTS #98 (the Deadpool issue), so it does not even begin in this run,
# and Vol. 02 opens on an early single-digit X-Force cover. Neither can reach a file we hold, because
# the lowest issue on this shelf is #43, so the refusals cost nothing.
#
# Note the two collection lines: the numbered "X-Force Vol. NN" trades and the separate "X-Force by
# Milligan and Allred" pair. They cover different parts of the run and do not overlap.
