# Shaman King (S15458) - 54 collected editions: the Kodansha 2020 complete run v01-v35, plus FIVE separate
# works filed on the same Series id (Flowers 6, Marcos 2, Red Crimson 4, The Super Star 5, Zero 2). No
# archive names a chapter on its pages, so the earlier manga sweep read nothing here and reported only
# "six works in one id".
#
# Every volume of the main run prints a numbered contents page ("CH. 9 The Shaman King  005") in its front
# matter, and all 35 were read. Its index moves - p002 in v01, p003 in v07/v10/v11/v15-v19/v21-v23, p004 in
# v02-v06/v08/v09/v12-v14/v20/v24-v26, p006 in v27-v35 - so where p004 held story or the character page the
# volume was re-opened at the index that held its contents. Nothing was inferred from a volume ordinal.
#
# The coordinate is the CHAPTER (PLAN.md 4.2). The ladder tiles with no gap and no overlap from c1 to c299
# over 34 volumes, every boundary attested twice.
#
# v35 is REFUSED on its own evidence, not for want of looking: its contents page lists "The Last Word",
# "Visionz 01"-"Visionz 05", a Shaman King Flowers prologue and two remix tracks, and NOT ONE numbered
# chapter. It is the bonus volume of the Kang Zeng Bang edition and has no range in the chapter coordinate.
#
# CONFLATION, not resolved here: the five spin-offs each restart their own chapter numbering at 1 (the
# sweep measured a v01 at c1-c4..c1-c8 for each), so any range for them would collide with Shaman King
# v01's own c1-c8 - PLAN.md 4.4's cross-run collision, inside one Series id. All 19 of their volumes are
# refused and the shelf is flagged.
#
# DATA DEFECT, not a containment question: item 43897, "Shaman King - Zero v02", has PageCount 0. Every
# other book on the shelf is 129-204pp. Either the archive is empty/unreadable or the scan never counted
# it; it is not something a range can describe either way.
