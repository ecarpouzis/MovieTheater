# THE COORDINATE (§4.2). The library holds no Hellboy floppies at all. The base run is the TWELVE
# numbered volumes, so every range here is in VOLUME numbers and the omnibuses are what nest over them.
# The twelve volumes themselves are refused: a range in this shelf numbering would only name themselves,
# and a range in issue numbers would mis-nest against a ladder that is not in issue numbers.
#
# WHAT THE OMNIBUSES ACTUALLY COLLECT — the shelf proves it twice over.
#
# 1. The four SHORT-STORY volumes were re-collected somewhere else. Vol. 03 The Chained Coffin (179pp),
#    Vol. 07 The Troll Witch (147), Vol. 10 The Crooked Man (162) and Vol. 11 The Bride of Hell (203)
#    total 691pp. `Hellboy - The Complete Short Stories` Vol. 01 + Vol. 02 (S66460) are 369 + 369 = 738pp.
#    That is those four volumes plus front matter, and it is why the omnibuses hold only the ARC volumes.
#
# 2. The page sums land on the arc volumes and nowhere else. Each volume carries ~10pp of front matter
#    that the omnibus drops:
#      Omni 02 (417pp): V4 147 + V5 147 + V6 154 = 448, less ~30 = 418.   <- 4-6, not 4-7
#      Omni 03 (529pp): V8 195 + V9 194 + V12 177 = 566, less ~30 = 536.  <- 8, 9 and 12
#      V10 + V11 (the two short-story volumes between them) are NOT in it.
#
# THE TWO §6.1 DEFECTS, AND WHAT THEY ARE.
#   Omnibus 03 stored #8-10. Its own note names 'Darkness Calls', 'The Wild Hunt' and 'The Storm and the
#   Fury' — and on THIS shelf The Storm and the Fury is Vol. 12, not the vol 10 the note's writer called
#   it. So the row claimed Vol. 10 (The Crooked Man), which is not in the book, and missed Vol. 12, which
#   is. Corrected to a bounding 8-12 whose note names the members, so 10 and 11 are excluded.
#
#   Omnibus 04 stored #11-12, claiming The Bride of Hell and The Storm and the Fury. It collects neither:
#   `Hellboy in Hell` Vol. 01 (153pp) and Vol. 02 (153pp) are Series 8764, and 153+153 less front matter
#   is this book's 288pp. Nothing it contains is numbered in this Series, so it is refused outright.
#
#   Omnibus 01 stored #1-3 and keeps only 1-2: 131+147 = 278 against 368pp leaves ~90pp, which is part of
#   Vol. 03 and not the whole 179pp of it. Claiming 3 would call a volume redundant that is only half in
#   the book, and over-claiming is the direction that loses files.
