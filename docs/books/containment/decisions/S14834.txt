# Rosario+Vampire Season II (S14834) — the complete 14-volume Viz edition of the second series,
# LostNerevarine-Empire digital rips. No loose issues on the shelf. (The first series is S14833, with its
# own `Test N` numbering that restarts at 1 — the two shelves must not be joined.)
#
# The archives are flat and name no chapter, so every volume was opened. Season II prints a CONTENTS
# panel over its cover art — the chapter number, a colon and the title — at p5 or p6 depending on how
# much front matter the volume carries:
#
#     36 : The Wounded ...7 / 37 : Reunion ...42 / 38 : Alliance ...78 / 39 : Scenes #1 ...114 /
#     40 : Scenes #2 ...140 / 41 : Bond ...166        ROSARIO+VAMPIRE Season II — Contents 9
#
# Nine volumes state both endpoints that way. Three (v11, v12, v13) print no contents panel where the
# others do, and their FIRST chapter's divider page carries the number instead — `48: Talent`,
# `54: His Goal`, `60: Dark Side of the Moon` — so those three are opened at their own divider and closed
# by the next volume's first chapter.
#
# The ladder tiles chapters 1 to 66 with no gap and no overlap.
#
# v14 is the finale and holds ONE numbered chapter: its contents panel reads `66 : Dawn of the Dark ...5`
# and `66.6 : Epilogue ...214`. That is not a volume-ordinal artefact (§6.2) — 66 is nowhere near this
# book's ordinal of 14 — it is a 209-page closing chapter followed by an epilogue, and the page numbers
# on its own contents panel say so.
