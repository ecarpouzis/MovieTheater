# Venom (S20988) - 14 collected editions, 78 loose files (55 of them one-page variant covers).
# Three ranges read off the books and stored; eleven refused, including one that was READ and is still
# refused because this shelf cannot hold it safely.
#
# The shelf carries THREE runs that all number from #1 or near it:
#   Venom v3 (2016)  Mike Costa - #1-6, then legacy-renumbered to #150-165.  We hold #159, #160, #162, #163.
#   Venom v4 (2018)  Donny Cates - we hold #31-34, the King in Black tie-ins, in the chronology tree.
#   Venom v5 (2021)  Al Ewing    - we hold #13 and #35-39, plus variant covers across #1-39.
#
# Marvel prints the answer on the CREDITS page (p2 in these rips), not in an indicia sentence: the modern
# trades attribute each artist to the issues they drew, and reading those attributions IS the range.
#
#   Venom by Al Ewing Vol. 06 - Infiltration    "(#26-28)", "(#29-30)", "(#27-28, #30)"      -> #26-30
#   Venom by Al Ewing Vol. 07 - Exsanguination  "VENOM #31-32", "VENOM #33-34", "CARNAGE #5-6" -> #31-34
#   Venom Vol. 01 - Homecoming (2017)           "GERARDO SANDOVAL (#1-6) ... RAMIREZ (#4) ... COELLO (#5)" -> #1-6
#   Venom Vol. 03 - Lethal Protector            "#154 SKIN DEEP", "#155-158 LETHAL PROTECTOR"  -> #154-158
#   Venom Vol. 04 - The Nativity                "#161 TANGLED WEBS", "#164-165 THE NATIVITY"   -> #161 AND #164-165, GAPPED
#
# The back page carries an ad with a "COLLECTING VENOM (20XX) #N-N" line, and on these Ewing volumes the
# ad matches the book's own credits - but it is an ADVERT, and on the Costa trades it advertises a
# different book entirely. Use the credits page; treat the back ad as corroboration only.
#
# TWO READ RANGES ARE REFUSED ANYWAY, which is the point of this file:
#
#   Vol. 07 - Exsanguination's #31-34 is correct and unusable. Venom (2018) #31-34 is ALSO on this shelf -
#   four real files (118507-118510, the King in Black tie-ins) and two variant covers - so the range would
#   nest six files from Donny Cates' run under Al Ewing's trade. Thirteen correct nestings do not pay for
#   six wrong ones (PLAN.md 2). It becomes storable the day the 2018 run is split off.
#
#   Vol. 01 - Homecoming's #1-6 is correct and unusable for the same reason: eleven one-page variant
#   covers of Venom (2021) #1-6 sit on this shelf and would go under a 2017 trade.
#
#   Vol. 04 - The Nativity is GAPPED - it holds #161 and #164-165 and NOT #162-163 - and #162 and #163
#   are exactly two of the four Costa issues we hold. The hull #161-165 would take both. Nothing is
#   stored; the reading is recorded here instead.
#
# LOCG had Vol. 06 - Infiltration at #31-34, which is Vol. 07's range, not Vol. 06's - off by one volume,
# and it was nesting twenty files including all four of the 2018 issues. LOCG's #1-30 on Vol. 05 is a hull.
# ComicVine's #154-158 on Vol. 03 agrees with what the book prints.
#
# The seven Epic Collections reprint 1990s Venom material - Lethal Protector, Carnage Unleashed, the
# Modern Era volumes - whose issues belong to other series entirely and are not in this library.
