# B-027 — the second Fantagraphics batch: 150 shelves, 143 of them one book filed under `Fantagraphics`
# or one of its author/series subfolders. Same shape as B-026 and the same default line: ComicVine's
# count-1 volume plus the Grand Comics Database's count-1 series, agreeing on name, publisher and year,
# with both legs item-level for a one-book series, so every book also gets its own `I` line.
#
# Findings worth the ledger (they extend B-026's):
#  (1) **GCD's subtitle is where the second leg hides — again, and harder here.** Eleven shelves looked
#      leg-less until the dump was probed on a distinctive FRAGMENT: "Roly Poly" -> 128114 "Roly Poly:
#      Phanta's Story"; "Twilight of the Assholes" -> 56923 "…: Cartoons & Essays 2005-2009"; "Wally
#      Gropius" -> 55391 "…: The Umpteen Millionaire"; "The Anthology of Mind" -> 149532 "…: Selected Short
#      Stories"; "Is This How You See Me?" -> 143584 "…(A Locas Story)"; "Tears of the Leather-Bound Saints"
#      -> 160036 "…(Tad Martin)"; "The Penguin Café…" -> 174797 (the é is what the exact probe choked on);
#      "Winter Warrior" -> 151646; "Pretty in Ink" -> 114595, whose subtitle ends **1896-2013** where
#      ComicVine's ends 1896–2010 — the same book, two catalogue dates. A GCD probe that returns nothing is
#      not evidence of absence until a fragment has been tried.
#  (2) **A shelf named by a bare title word collects strangers.** "Golden Age" pulled a 12pp Superman
#      webrip onto Willard Mullin's baseball book; "Orion" put Walt Simonson's DC omnibus on Masamune
#      Shirow's Dark Horse manga; "The Eternaut" swallowed its own sequel "The Eternaut 1969". Each is
#      `F misfiled` with the intruder given its own `I` line.
#  (3) **Four more EC Artists' Library volumes** (High Cost of Dying #15, Thing from the Grave #19, Three
#      for the Money #31, Death Stand #22) — handled exactly as in B-026: ComicVine volume on the S line,
#      GCD issue id + ISBN on the `I` line, no gcd= on the S line so the three shelves are not merged into
#      GCD's single 39-volume series 69863. Seven such shelves now across the two batches.
#  (4) **Krazy & Ignatz is the Epic Collection shape in its purest form**: GCD keeps ONE series (56929,
#      thirteen volumes 1916-1944) where ComicVine mints a volume per book, and the stored link is a
#      DIFFERENT reprint line entirely (54447, Pacific Comics Club, 3 volumes). Our thirteen files map one
#      to one onto GCD's thirteen issues by their year ranges — the tightest arithmetic in the batch.
#  (5) **Two shelves are the same book twice**: S7896 and S20337 are both "Green Eggs and Maakies" (CV
#      64716 / GCD 75695), one filed in `Maakies` and one loose — `F merge-with`.
#  (6) Seven shelves are genuinely absent from GCD (`provider-missing`) and three carry a stored ComicVine
#      link that is a FOREIGN-language edition of the right book (Alison -> Errata Naturae, The Clitoris ->
#      Barbara Fiore, Totem -> a Serbian Maverick series); on those the GCD row is the only honest leg.
#
# `I` lines: one per BOOK, naming every item it covers when a shelf holds the same rip twice.
