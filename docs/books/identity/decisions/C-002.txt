R 10002 | The dominant body of this shelf — roughly 1,720 of 1,747 files in the "Judge Dredd Part 01..08" folders — is a fan-made reading-order extraction of the Dredd strip out of 2000 AD progs (5-25pp fragments named by prog number and story title, plus "(Colour Reprint)" twins of most of them), which the pass treats as not a published edition and never links; the 2000 AD weekly those pages came from is already S37 (CV 19752 / GCD 11289+11293+11294+11295) and linking this shelf there would fuse 1,747 strip fragments into the weekly. The remaining ~27 files are FOUR different published comics, each with its own records and none spanning the rest: IDW's 2012 ongoing (CV 54111 / GCD 70025, 30 issues) held only as its collected line CV 34817 / GCD 73628 (7 books, our Vol. 01-07 plus two duplicate rips); IDW's Judge Dredd: Year One mini (CV 59192 / GCD 72940, 4 issues) held only as the TPB CV 68529 / GCD 223522, ripped three times; Rebellion's Essential Judge Dredd line (7 books, ComicVine minting a volume per book 129860/133318/138336/143600/144275/152641/159731, GCD holding only 181497 and 223094); and IDW's Judge Dredd: 100-Page Giant 2020 (CV 125630 / GCD 157474). Every candidate that could look "spanning" was rejected on arithmetic: CV 3284 (Eagle 1983, 35), 3928 (Fleetway Quality 1987, 61), 19045 (DC 1994, 18), 59215 (Titan 1988, 5), 127476 (JuniorPress 1984, 7) and GCD 50902 (Titan 1981-1989, 26), 2809 (Eagle 1983-1986, 33) are American/Dutch/Swedish reprint magazines of a few dozen issues against a 1,747-file shelf whose ladder runs to 1824, and the per-file GCD leg reached only 7+1+1 files.
F 10002 split-needed | proposed runs: (1) the ~1,720 prog story-extracts — no published record, leave unlinked; (2) IDW Judge Dredd 2012 ongoing/collected — cv=34817 gcd=73628 for the seven trades we hold (items 106359-106365, plus duplicate rips 106377/106378); (3) Judge Dredd: Year One — cv=68529 gcd=223522 for the TPB (items 4482, 30893, 106380); (4) Essential Judge Dredd — a ComicVine volume per book, cv=129860/133318/138336/143600/144275/152641/159731 for items 4486-4492; (5) Judge Dredd: 100-Page Giant — cv=125630 gcd=157474 for item 30894. Splitting on the FOLDER does it: Judge Dredd Part 01..08 is (1), The Essential Judge Dredd is (4), Misc\Judge Dredd (2013) + IDW\Judge Dredd + Judge Dredd TPBs are (2)/(3)/(5).
F 10002 not-a-run | the eight "Judge Dredd Part NN" folders are a ripper's reading order over 46 years of a weekly anthology, not a publishing run: the numbers in the filenames are 2000 AD PROG numbers (ladder 1-1824 with hundreds of gaps), several files carry a letter suffix for an annual or special ("#114b (2000AD Sci-Fi Special 1979)", "#128c (Dan Dare Annual 1980)", "#175f (Judge Dredd Annual 1981) Max Normal"), and two files are not even Dredd strips (Max Normal, Walter The Wobot).
N 10002 run (2) IDW's 2012 ongoing: cv=54111 gcd=70025 (30 issues, 2012-2015) is the run; we hold none of its floppies, only the collected line cv=34817 gcd=73628 (7 books in both providers, our seven volumes matching volume-for-volume).
N 10002 run (3) Judge Dredd: Year One: cv=59192 gcd=72940 (4 issues, IDW 2013) is the run; we hold only the collection, cv=68529 gcd=223522 (count 1 in both providers), at 104-105pp in three separate rips.
N 10002 run (4) Essential Judge Dredd (Rebellion 2020-2024): cv=- gcd=- at series level — ComicVine mints a volume per BOOK (the Epic Collection shape) and GCD indexes only two of the seven, so the line has no single spanning record either; each book takes its own I line.
N 10002 run (5) Judge Dredd: 100-Page Giant: cv=125630 gcd=157474, IDW 2020, count 1 in both providers, our rip 100pp — the title's own page count.
N 10002 the "(Colour Reprint)" twins are the 1990s colour recolouring of the same strips, 1-3pp shorter than the mono original each time (e.g. #110 Punks Rule 7pp vs 8pp, #131-132 Sob Story 13pp vs 14pp) — two editions of one strip, which is why 255 prog numbers name more than one file. They are not a second run.
