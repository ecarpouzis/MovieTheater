# Two house shapes dominate. Fantagraphics is the cleanest in any batch so far — a single Fantagraphics CV
# volume and a single Fantagraphics GCD series agreeing on publisher, span and count on 24 of 25 shelves.
# EUROPE COMICS is the hard one and is a new convention: Europe Comics is a digital-only ENGLISH imprint of
# Dargaud / Dupuis / Le Lombard / Norma, so ComicVine has a Europe Comics volume for every shelf and GCD has
# NO Europe Comics row at all. Where the dump offers exactly one original-language row whose span and album
# count match our files I take it and say so in the clause (Dargaud Bootblack, Harlem, Slava, NeoForest,
# Dalí, Norma's Contrapaso, Le Lombard's Karmela Krimm) at 0.9. Where GCD holds TWO rows for the same
# original publisher — the French and the Dutch-language editions, which the dump does not distinguish
# (Le Lombard's Irons, Jakob Kayne, Purple Heart, Green Class; Dupuis' Atom Agency) — I write gcd=- and
# F provider-missing rather than guess between them. That choice is the lead's to confirm.
# I-line convention as before: a book gets an I line only when its own record differs from the S line's, and
# every I here is gcd=s<series> except one ISBN read off a barcode (Battling Boy).
