# A-030 — 136 shelves in four blocks: the back half of Scout Comics (and Black Caravan), the Silver Sprocket
# and Source Point Press folders, the whole Titan Comics folder (Doctor Who, Blade Runner, Elric, Robotech,
# Rivers of London and two dozen minis), and the Valiant folder including _Acclaim and _Chronological Valiant.
# The Titan Doctor Who sub-folder is the batch's most instructive shape. Each "Year Two / Year Three" folder
# is its own ComicVine volume and its own GCD series, and the numbering is continuous across a Year (Year Two
# is #6-15, Year Three #1-13/14) — so a ladder starting at 6 is not a gap, it is the Year. Beside them sits
# `Doctor Who - The Lost Dimension (2017)`, a read-order crossover folder that holds a SECOND rip of one issue
# from three different Year Three runs (Tenth #9, Eleventh #10, Twelfth #8) plus three genuine one-shots
# (Alpha, Omega, The Ninth Special). That folder is the source of every ratio just above 1.00 here, and it is
# also why all three of its one-shots were handed the Tenth Doctor Year Three series by `round2-folder` — a
# leg rejected three times, with the dump's exact-title rows (116156, 118118, 117417) taken instead.
# Ghost Stories is the digital-chapter shape: eight 16pp files where the print run is four issues, so
# `gcd=-` + provider-missing rather than a count that lies.
# Trade-only shelves again: SEVEN stored CV links are the count-1 collected record while both legs carry the
# run — Mindbender (111434 over 101400/114193), Star Bastard (129318 over 117997/143431), The Source (122696
# over 114441/144041), Zinnober (117484 over 111575/131884), Ogre (117982 over 114439/134554), Scarlett
# Couture (86990 over 81618/88763) and Solar Flare (106546 over the six-issue GCD 113526). One more foreign
# link: The Impure's stored CV 141675 is Cross Cult's German edition of a Scout book.
# One refusal: S14714 Rivers of London is ELEVEN different Titan minis, each collected as one numbered volume
# of a single line; the stored link describes only "Body Work" (volume 1).
# NEW CONVENTIONS for the ledger: (a) Titan's Doctor Who "Year N" volumes number continuously across the
# whole Doctor, so a shelf whose ladder starts at 6 is complete, not partial; (b) a read-order crossover
# folder inside a publisher's own tree (Titan's Lost Dimension, Valiant's _Chronological Valiant) behaves
# exactly like DC's `#DC Events` — a second rip plus round2-folder contamination; (c) Valiant's Acclaim-era
# and Alien Books-era shelves are the cleanest in the library: 20 of them are 0.95 with nothing to say.
# I-line convention as in A-016..A-029: a book gets an I line only when its own record is different from or
# finer than the S line's.
