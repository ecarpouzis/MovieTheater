# B-073 — tier B's tail, and it is the same folder B-072 ended in: `_Marvel Major Event Chronology (digital)`
# from Spider-Geddon (2018) to Blood Hunt (2024), one folder per tie-in holding that tie-in's collected
# edition. The same ruling decides all 52: where both providers hold the numbered mini behind the trade, the
# S line is the run and the count-1 collected record goes on the book's I line; where the book gathers arcs
# of an ongoing or several different series and both legs mint only the count-1 record, that record IS the
# comic. Modern Marvel has made the artefact almost total — the stored link is the count-1 trade on 26 of
# these 52 shelves, and every single Absolute Carnage, Devil's Reign and King in Black tie-in here carries it.
# One shelf is six different comics (S24281, the six Age of X-Man minis in one folder) and takes an R.
# The GCD ISBNs and page counts placed every trade: modern Marvel tie-in collections are 116pp almost without
# exception, which is what a three-issue mini becomes, and our rips land 4-12 pages under that every time.
