# B-024 — three folders: the tail of Dark Horse's "EC Comics & Others Archives" (20 shelves), a small
# Eclipse/Epic block (10), and then 120 shelves of the Europe Comics digital imprint, A through L.
#
# The archives corner splits in two, and the split is the interesting finding. On most of these shelves BOTH
# legs hold the ARCHIVE line (CV "The EC Archives: X" + a GCD Dark Horse collected-edition row) and nothing
# holds the 1950s EC comic — so the archive line is the identity at 0.9 with an N naming the comic it reprints.
# But on Saddle Justice and Shock Illustrated (and, in B-023, Crime Illustrated and Crime Patrol) BOTH legs hold
# the ORIGINAL EC series instead, and the archive volume is only a printing — so the S line takes the 1948/1955
# EC series and the I line takes the Dark Horse book we hold. Which shape you get is decided by what the packet's
# CV link happens to be, not by anything about the comic; the lead may want these harmonised.
#
# Europe Comics is the batch's bulk and its own shape, exactly as the ledger says: ComicVine mints one volume per
# Europe Comics title and GCD holds NO Europe Comics rows at all. Per Eric's ruling I link the original-language
# GCD row where the dump surfaces one (Dargaud, Dupuis, Le Lombard, Ballon, Standaard, Silvester — 26 shelves),
# naming the language, at 0.9. Where the dump holds nothing for the title — 88 shelves — it is an honest
# provider-missing at 0.9 on the ComicVine leg alone. NOTHING in this folder can reach 0.95.
# Two more Europe Comics observations: several of these titles have an ENGLISH print edition in GCD that is not
# a translation at all (SelfMadeHero, Cinebook, NBM, Fanfare, Magnetic Press, Ablaze) — those are the same
# English text under another imprint and are the better second leg; and the CV per-file matcher put a French
# Soleil volume ("Elle" 2010) on the Elle(s) shelf by dropping the "(s)".
