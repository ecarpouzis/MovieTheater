# A-017 — 126 shelves: the tail of IDW (Strawberry Shortcake through Yo-Kai Watch), a short Icon/Jinxworld
# block, and then Image from '68: Last Rites to Fell. Two publishers, two completely different shelf shapes.
# IDW here is the same house shape A-016 described: a 2-6 issue mini whose filenames carry "(of NN)", a CV
# volume and a GCD series agreeing on IDW, the year and the count, and a complete ladder — those are 0.95 and
# there are about twenty of them.
# Image is where the work is. Image's digital shelves are overwhelmingly TRADE-ONLY (one or more "COL … Vol. NN"
# files and no issues at all): 45 of the 96 Image shelves in this batch. Every one of them shows the same three
# records — the RUN (CV volume + GCD dump row, both carrying the issue count), a GCD "Collected Series /
# collected edition" row whose count equals the BOOKS we hold, and a CV volume with count 1 named after the
# trade's subtitle. The "Trades vs run" ruling settles all of them: the S line is the RUN, the collected row
# goes on the books' own I lines. Nine shelves had the stored CV link pointing at the count-1 trade record
# (Crude, Death Vigil, Death of Love, Fairlady, D4VE, Apocalypse Al, Supermarket, Blood Squad Seven, Deep
# Beyond) — F wrong-cv-link on each.
# What surprised me: the GCD "collected" row and the GCD "run" row are almost never the same id, and the
# per-file leg picks the COLLECTED one on a trade-only shelf (it matched the books, which is exactly the
# "matches our holdings" shape) while the dump names the run. Reading the dump line rather than the per-file
# line is what decided 40+ of these shelves.
# Also: S985 and S986 are the SAME folder (Image\Analog (2018)) split into two shelves because Vol. 01's
# parsed key carries the subtitle — F merge-with.
# I-line convention as in A-016: a book gets an I line only when its own record differs from what the S line
# carries, and no ComicVine ITEM-level issue ids appear anywhere in this packet, so every I line is
# gcd=s<series> or nothing.
