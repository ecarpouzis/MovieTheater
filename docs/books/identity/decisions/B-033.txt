# SelfMadeHero. Same house shape as B-032: one book, one CV count-1 volume, one GCD count-1 series with
# the same publisher and year -> two legs, 0.95, plus the book's own `I` line.
# Conventions this batch taught:
#  * NBM is the most completely indexed publisher in either provider — 40 of 43 NBM shelves paired
#    cleanly, the "… in Comics!" biography line included; only Buja's Diary, Elvis and An Enchantment
#    are absent from the GCD dump.
#  * Scholastic/Graphix is the opposite of NBM: GCD mints a SERIES PER BOOK (Animorphs, Banana Fox,
#    I Survived's later volumes) or TWIN rows per book (Drama, Guts, Hey Kiddo, Space Dumplins, Positively
#    Izzy, Baby-Sitters Club), and the twin is separated by our rip's PAGE COUNT more often than not.
#  * ComicVine mints a volume per BOOK on Scholastic's long lines while GCD keeps one series (Dog Man:
#    CV 93629 is a count-1 stub, GCD 106235 runs to 14) — the Cinebook shape, inverted from Animorphs
#    where ComicVine spans and GCD splits. Whichever provider spans carries the S line.
#  * SelfMadeHero's English editions are in ComicVine but GCD often files them [en-gb] under the same
#    title one year later; "FUN" and "Hair Shirt" are indexed by BOTH providers only in the German
#    avant-verlag edition, so the per-file matcher picks a German volume for an English rip.
#  * Two shelves in this batch are ONE book split by a filename (Love Me Please! S11000/S11001) and two
#    more are one line split by a subfolder (Baby-Sitters Club S1758/S18048); `merge-with` on both pairs.
