# Will Eisner shelf and the Tintin album line. The house shape here is: ONE file, ONE CV count-1 volume,
# ONE GCD count-1 series with the same publisher and year — two legs, 0.95, and the book's own record is
# the shelf's record, so nearly every shelf also gets its `I` line.
# Conventions this batch taught (all verified, not guessed):
#  * LeDuch's Avery Hill rips put a ComicVine ISSUE id in ComicInfo `Web` (Ismyre 703785, Terrible Means
#    709592, The Tower in the Sea 723694 — 3/3 resolve to the stored volume's only issue) = the 1.0 shape,
#    the Marika-Empire shape on a second ripper.
#  * Abrams ComicArts is indexed by BOTH providers with the same year and title on 24 of 25 shelves; the
#    packet's "CV candidates" block is pure noise there ("Art of Abrams", "The Discworld Graphic Novels",
#    "Graphic Novels" from Süddeutsche Zeitung recur on ~40 shelves and never mean anything).
#  * Lerner/Graphic Universe mints TWIN GCD rows per book (library-bound vs paperback); the page count in
#    the rip picks one where GCD records it, and where both rows are blank the line is capped at 0.9.
#  * The GCD dump is missing whole imprints here: HarperAlley 2022-24, Andrews McMeel one-offs, Abrams'
#    Magic: The Gathering art books, Lerner's translated album lines (Mortensen, Tao, Little Prince has
#    one). `provider-missing` on the GCD side is the single commonest honest answer in this batch.
#  * Ralf König's English editions are split three ways: Catalan Communications 1992, "Ignite
#    Entertainment" 2009-2011 (GCD only) and Northwest Press 2016-2017 (ComicVine only).
