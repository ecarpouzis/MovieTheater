# carrying both item-level ids and the ISBN GCD prints.

# Findings worth the ledger:
#  (1) **Eleven RUN-wins**, every one with the stored (or per-file) ComicVine link on the count-1 trade
#      record a year later: Crush (CV 22506 / GCD 31647, Dark Horse's four 2003 issues, not the 2004 TPB),
#      Happy! (52401 / 68826), Heart (43809 / 64464), Mara (55037 / 70552), Muirwood: The Lost Abbey
#      (83831 / 91358), Scarlet in Gaslight (26974 / 16337), Skyscrapers of the Midwest (64094 / 60965),
#      Flesh of White (94116 / 106504), Dead Legends (121844), Dry (123036) and Silence (88249). The Image
#      three-record trap fires exactly as the ledger says on Happy!, Heart and Mara — the per-file GCD leg
#      and the per-file CV link BOTH name the collected record, and their agreement is not two legs.
#  (2) **The GCD dump hides behind the SUBTITLE, again.** "Giraffes on Horseback Salad: Salvador Dali, the
#      Marx Brothers, and the Strangest Movie Never Made" returns nothing; "Giraffes on Horseback Salad"
#      alone is GCD 142553 (Quirk Books 2019). Same shape as B-029's Hunting Accident. Always re-probe
#      the short title before writing `provider-missing`.
#  (3) **A ripper's filename can carry the PUBLISHER's title shape.** Two H. G. Wells shelves (S8229,
#      S8230) are 2018 Mr Norrell-Empire rips named "H. G. Wells - <title>", which is exactly how Lion
#      Forge's and Insight Editions' 2018 lines title their books; both stored links are the older Campfire
#      2010 / Dark Horse 2005 adaptations of the same text. Flagged `wrong-cv-link` and re-linked at 0.9.
#  (4) **Three shelves hold TWO DIFFERENT COMICS that share a title**: Heart of Darkness (SelfMadeHero's
#      2010 adaptation and W. W. Norton's 2019 Peter Kuper one — refused and split), Leaf (Fantagraphics
#      2015 and Fair Spark 2019) and Queen Bee (Scholastic's 2005 book and Kodansha's 2019 manga "Queen
#      Bee!"). Each intruder still gets its own `I` line so the book is identified even where the shelf is not.
#  (5) **An EAN13 barcode read off the page is the strongest assertion in this batch** — S7854's
#      9781845297107 is exactly GCD issue 814747's ISBN (Constable & Robinson's UK printing of The Mammoth
#      Book of Best Crime Comics), which is a 1.0 on the `I` line even though the S line takes the US
#      Running Press row ComicVine names.
#  (6) 46 shelves take `gcd=-` + `provider-missing` at 0.9: Retrofit, Inverse Press, AAM/Markosia, A Wave
#      Blue World, Consumptive Press and the other American small presses are systematically absent from
