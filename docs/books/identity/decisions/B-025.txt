# B-025 — 150 shelves, and 146 of them are ONE folder: Europe Comics, the digital English imprint that
# publishes Dargaud / Dupuis / Le Lombard / Glénat albums. The tier-B shape is the same on almost every
# shelf: ComicVine holds a Europe Comics volume whose count equals our ladder exactly (the rips are
# 45-160pp albums, one album per file, numbered in reading order), and the Grand Comics Database holds NO
# Europe Comics rows at all. So the second leg has to be the SAME comic in another edition, and this batch
# found it three ways, in this order of preference:
#   (1) an ENGLISH PRINT sister edition — Cinebook, Titan, Nobrow, Fantagraphics: the same text, not a
#       translation, and a real second leg (0.95 when the counts also agree): Memories from the Civil War
#       (Cinebook 185274), Miss Endicott (185268), Mutations (185503), Wyoming Doll (163305), Nowhere Girl
#       (Nobrow 190651), The Extraordinary (Titan 173267), Spirou & Fantasio (Cinebook 57408).
#   (2) the ORIGINAL-LANGUAGE row (Eric's ruling: the right comic in the wrong language), at 0.9 with the
#       language named: Medina (Le Lombard 53908), Otaku Blue (Dargaud 132460), Shelley (126637), Rampokan
#       (Dupuis 148385), Tanz! (Le Lombard 220154), Trap (Dargaud 167643), Zaroff (158891), Nocéan (222834),
#       Omni-visibilis (83344), Le temps des Mitaines (219531), Les souris de Leningrad (150208), Soon
#       (157288), Raj (Dargaud 120583), Malaterre (Sherpa 195526).
#   (3) nothing — `gcd=-` + `F provider-missing`, at 0.9 on the ComicVine volume plus our own naming, years
#       and count. That is 111 of the 150 shelves and it is the honest answer, not a gap in the reading.
#
# Findings worth the ledger:
#  (1) **The accent is the whole miss.** The packet probes the ENGLISH title, so a French original with a
#      diacritic is invisible: "Nocean" returns nothing, "Nocéan" returns GCD 222834 (Dupuis 2023, 3). The
#      ComicVine candidate list is where the accented spelling is sitting — re-probe the dump with it.
#  (2) A Europe Comics shelf whose ComicVine volume is NOT a Europe Comics volume is the tell that the comic
#      is somebody else's: S18546's three albums are Titan's ExtraOrdinary (CV 135879 / GCD 173267, both
#      count 5) and S19888's four 30pp floppies are IDW's The Veil (CV 26723 / GCD 39852, both count 4) —
#      the "(Europe Comics)" in those filenames is the ripper's tag, not the publisher.
#  (3) S16312's stored CV 27781 is Dupuis' Spirou MAGAZINE (4,351 issues) hung on two Cinebook albums — the
#      biggest hull in the batch. The albums are Cinebook's Spirou & Fantasio #19-20.
#  (4) ComicVine mints a volume per BOOK on several of these lines (Menji, Yojimbot, Story Without a Hero,
#      The Grande Odalisque) exactly as it does on Cinebook and Epic Collection lines.
