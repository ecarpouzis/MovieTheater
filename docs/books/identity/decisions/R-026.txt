# R-026 — the withheld-PAIR revisit. Eleven shelves whose reader found the right ComicVine id but could not
# store it because a partner shelf already held it, read together with those partners, plus Wolverine (1989)
# and the three Friday shelves. Each pair came out one of three ways: (a) the partner is the SAME comic — a
# second rip, a folder split, a trade of the run — and the two merge; (b) the partner is a DIFFERENT or a
# MIXED comic wrongly holding the id, so it takes wrong-cv-link and the id goes to the shelf that earned it;
# (c) the id is genuinely the partner's own identity and the withheld claim is refused.
# Conventions this batch showed: a shelf that is only a 1pp Skottie Young / variant-cover rip IS the mini it
# covers (merge-with when the mini's own shelf exists, run ids + partial-rip when it does not); a
# "_Minis & One Shots" or "_Deluxe Editions" bucket filed beside a numbered run is never that run's volume,
# and the stored link that says it is, is the commonest shape here (4 of 11 pairs); two shelves split by a
# possessive ("Tick" vs "Tick's") or by a subtitle in one filename ("Nottingham - The Final Tithe") are one
# comic; and a GCD publisher-era split (Deadworld Arrow 9 + Caliber 17 = ComicVine's 26) is arithmetic that
# confirms the spanning CV volume rather than contradicting it.
