#
# Findings worth the ledger:
#  (1) **A DIACRITIC and a SUBTITLE are still the two ways GCD hides.** "Safe Area Gorazde" returns nothing;
#      "Goražde" returns 57151, the Special Edition our file actually is. "Seeds and Stems" returns nothing;
#      "Seeds and Stems: Contains Outtakes and Rare Shorts" is GCD 162384. Also Fab4 Mania -> 125792 "…: A
#      Beatles Obsession and the Concert of a Lifetime", Rebel Visions -> 88788 "…1963-1975", Shattuck ->
#      102357 "…[Original Art Edition]", Sock Monkey Treasury -> 80506 "…: A Tony Millionaire's Sock Monkey
#      Collection", Megg & Mogg -> 115799 "…and Other Stories" (no colon).
#  (2) **GCD keeps ONE series per LINE where ComicVine mints a volume per book — five more lines here.**
#      The Alberto Breccia Library (GCD 132818, six volumes) alone accounts for FIVE shelves: Mort Cinder #1,
#      Perramus #2, Dracula #4, Life of Che #5, Evita #6 (and B-027's Eternaut 1969 is its #3). The Zippy
#      Annual (56864, eleven) holds both Zippy shelves as #5 and #11. Peanuts Every Sunday (78271, ten) holds
#      both Peanuts shelves. The Complete Crumb Comics (34754) holds both Crumb shelves. The EC Artists'
#      Library (69863) takes six more shelves here. As in B-026 I keep gcd off those S lines rather than
#      merge unrelated books, and put the per-book GCD ISSUE id (with its ISBN) on the `I` line — except
#      where the shelf IS the whole line (Peanuts S13671, Crumb S4419), where the series id is right.
#  (3) **A creator folder splits one line into sibling shelves** — Peanuts Every Sunday v01-v08 vs v10, the
#      Complete Crumb v01-v17 vs the two Fritz the Cat volumes, the Freak Brothers' Follies volumes,
#      Cosplayers. Four `F merge-with` lines say so.
#  (4) **The bare-title collision keeps producing intruders**: "Girls" put the EC Artists' Library's *50
#      Girls 50* on Image's 24-issue Girls; "Displacement" put First Second's 2020 book on Fantagraphics'
#      2015 one; "Late Bloomer" put Retrofit's 2016 comic on Carol Tyler's 2005 book; "Petey & Pussy" pulled
#      in its own 2018 sequel. Each is `F misfiled` with the intruder given its own `I` line.
#  (5) **Two more RUN-wins**: Palestine (our 279pp collection over CV 67486 / GCD 26994, the nine-issue
#      1993 run) and Prison Pit (the Complete Collection over CV 53965 / GCD 54398, six volumes).
#  (6) One shelf is refused (Call Me Iggy — First Second 2024, in neither provider under any spelling,
#      with a stored link to an unrelated 2001 Sound & Vision book) and one is split (Cosplayers' second
#      shelf, two different one-shots).
#
