# THE SHAPE OF THIS WHOLE BATCH is the one the ledger names for Dynamite and IDW, and it holds for
# AWA Upshot, Ablaze, Ahoy, Action Lab and Albatross without a single exception I could find: for
# every title BOTH providers carry TWO records — the N-issue RUN, and a same-titled count-1 record
# minted a year later, which is the TRADE. Our file is almost always one 90-250pp collected book, so
# the "run wins" ruling applies on nearly every shelf: the S line takes the run's pair, and the trade
# gets its own I line on the count-1 GCD series. The stored CV link is the TRADE record on 14 shelves
# here (Archangel 8, Black Tape, Casual Fling, Chariot, Little Black Book, Marjorie Finnegan, Red
# Border, Redemption, Eros/Psyche, Bigfoot, Aero-Girl, Dog of Wonder, Archon, Zorro Man of the Dead)
# — a third of the trade-only shelves, exactly the rate the ledger predicts.
#
# TWO OTHER CLUSTERS worth adding to the ledger:
#   1. A sequel in a "v2 - <Subtitle>" / "vN (YYYY)" subfolder is its OWN CV volume and its OWN GCD
#      series, and the per-file link carries v1's ids across to it every time (Un-Sacred v2, My Bad v2,
#      American Mythology Monsters v3, Grumble v2, Year Zero v0). Each of those needed a fresh lookup
#      by the subtitle: CV 132245 / GCD 167803, CV 146702 / GCD 193482 "My Bad Vol. 2", CV 144299 /
#      GCD 187847, CV 126027 / GCD 158278, CV 145504 / GCD 190492 "Year Zero Vol. 0".
#   2. GCD simply has no row for a large minority of small-press American floppies — 34 shelves here
#      carry F provider-missing after an exact AND substring probe of the dump. Action Lab and
#      Aftershock miniseries are the worst (Bunny Mask, Kaiju Score, Maniac of New York, Undone By
#      Blood, Herald, Ruin of Thieves, Lucifer's Knight all absent). That is a coverage gap in the
#      dump, not a reading failure, and it is why so many lines here are 0.9 rather than 0.95.
#
