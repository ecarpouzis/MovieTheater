# B-037 — 150 tier-B shelves of the IDW folder, Godzilla through Star Trek. The house shape here is the
# IDW trade trap the ledger predicts at scale: a 100-160pp `COL`/one-file shelf whose stored ComicVine
# link is the same-title COUNT-1 record minted a year after the mini, with the real run sitting one line
# below it in "CV candidates" and a matching count-N row in the GCD dump. I found 31 of them and every
# one is `wrong-cv-link` + the run on the S line + the trade on an `I` line.
#
# Findings worth the ledger:
#  (1) **GCD prints the trade's PAGE COUNT and ISBN, and that is the strongest per-book check in the
#      batch.** Reading `gcd_issue` for the count-1 row matched our rip within ±10pp on 40+ books
#      (Godzilla in Hell 120/120, Parker's Getaway Face 28/28, Popeye Classics vol-by-vol 232/224,
#      200/192, 280/273 …). It also DISAMBIGUATED GCD's two "Hot Damn" count-1 rows: 155948 is an 8pp
#      ASHCAN, 161217 is the 120pp trade — not a twin pair at all.
#  (2) **A 1.0.** S7371's file carries an EAN13 read off the page, 9781600106453, and GCD issue 763424
#      (IDW; Yoe Books, January 2010, 130pp) prints ISBN 978-1600106453 — the same number. Both legs and
#      our own barcode name one book.
#  (3) **The Cinebook shape inside IDW.** GCD keeps ONE series "Godzilla Rivals" (195569) whose four
#      issues ARE the four trade volumes (Round One…Round Four, with ISBNs), while ComicVine mints a
#      volume per book. Three of our shelves are one GCD line → `merge-with` per the ledger's Grandville
#      rule. GCD does the same with "My Little Pony Annual" (77394 = the 2013, 2014 and 2017 annuals).
#  (4) **GCD is systematically blind to IDW's licensed kids' digests.** Not one of the "My Little Pony
#      (Animated)" volumes (Twilight's Kingdom, The Cutie Map, The Cutie Re-Mark, The Crystalling, To
#      Where and Back Again, Star Pupil) or "Art Is Magic!" is in the dump; ComicVine has a count-1
#      volume for each, and its ISSUE NAMES ("Volume 8", "Vol. 9", "Vol. 11", "Volume 12", "Volume 13")
#      line up one-for-one with our `COL … Vol. NN` filenames. That is what identified them.
#  (5) **Five shelves are several comics and get `R` + `split-needed`**: Goosebumps (one Scholastic
#      Graphix book + four separate IDW minis' trades), Silent Hill (four unrelated IDW minis),
#      My Little Pony Holiday Special (2015 and 2019 = two records in both providers — the Archie
#      holiday-annual shape), My Little Pony: Equestria Girls (2016 book + the 2020 March Radness
#      one-shot) and My Little Pony (Animated) (four digest volumes). Every intruder still gets its `I`.
#  (6) **Three reprint editions where the ORIGINAL wins** (the EC-Archives ruling): Sachs & Violens
#      (our 126pp file is IDW's 2016 collection of Marvel/Epic's 1993 four-issue series, CV 5042 / GCD
#      4728), Rebel (IDW 2009 edition of Pepe Moreno's 1986 Catalan album, CV 47389 / GCD 3252) and
#      Star Trek: First Contact (our 52pp file is Marvel's 1996 52pp adaptation, CV 19535 / GCD 5561,
#      re-released by IDW in 2021). Each keeps the printing we hold on its `I` line.
#  (7) The per-file ComicVine matcher reached a FOREIGN licence three times on bare words — Silent Hill
#      → Panini Verlag's German edition (62122), Sonic & Tails: Best Buds Forever → De Vrijbuiter's 1991
#      Dutch "Sonic the Hedgehog" (140313), and it stamped the 2022 "My Little Pony" ongoing (143628) on
#      four digest volumes and a 2017 annual. Rejected on publisher and on the page count.
