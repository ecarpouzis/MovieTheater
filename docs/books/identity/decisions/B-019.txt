# book on the shelf gets its own I line on the trade's GCD issue — that is where nearly all 120 I lines

# He-Man toys, Umbrella Academy making-of, Sakai Project, 35 Years of Covers, WoW Chronicle, Korra art) are

# prefix ("Edgar Rice Burroughs' Tarzan: …", 6309), Zero Day Exploit behind a plural ("Terminators:", 206141),
# every Korra book behind "Nickelodeon …", the FCBD 2021 book behind GCD's bracketed contents list.
