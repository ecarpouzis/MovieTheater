# R-005 — the 58 shelves of withheld.txt, read as 26 pairs plus six single re-links. In every pair one shelf
# had its ComicVine volume found and withheld because the id was the STORED link of a neighbouring, undecided
# shelf; this file gives the withheld shelf its volume on the S line and decides the partner on its own
# evidence. Thirteen partners take a different volume (the stored link was simply the wrong comic); eight are
# buckets or flagged conflated and take R + split-needed; four are the SAME comic as a shelf that already
# holds the volume and say so with merge-with (22070->22079 Worlds' Finest 2012, 64534->64535 the Deadman
# collected line, 66030->66031 the 2009 Outsiders, 17277->100142 the 2019 Jimmy Olsen).
#
# Findings worth the ledger:
#  (1) GCD spells DC's 2010-11 Batman Incorporated "Batman, Inc." (53178, 8 issues) — every packet that probed
#      "Batman Incorporated" reported no US 2011 row and was wrong; the abbreviation is the whole miss.
#  (2) A GCD STORY-title probe settles a v3-vs-v4 argument no name lookup can: S64893's two filenames
#      ("Storm Over Thanagar ... The Way of the Warrior Part 3", "Essential Warfare ... Part 6") are GCD
#      stories 566182 and 566960, both in Hawkman (1993) #22-23 (July/August 1995) — the shelf's recorded
#      years 2004-2004 are metadata noise, not the comic.
#  (3) The DC #DC Events read-order folders are the source of nearly every wrong stored link in this wave:
#      S3007, S7927, S10137, S16802, S22070, S58099, S64893, S65436 are all event-folder shelves whose bare
#      title reached a same-named run from another decade.
#  (4) ComicVine 156493's single issue is titled "World's Finest, Part 1/2/3" — which is why S20471 holds two
#      differently-named rips ("Trinity Special 001" and "Trinity Special - World's Finest 001") of ONE book.
#  (5) 21 of the 26 withheld ids were right on the first reading; not one had to be withdrawn.
