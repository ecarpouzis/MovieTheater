# The batch is dominated by AC Comics, and AC is the easiest publisher in the collection to read:
# one folder per title, "<Title> NNN (YYYY).cbz", a ComicInfo Publisher of "AC Comics", and a GCD
# series whose YearBegan-YearEnded span is literally the two years in the folder name. Where CV and
# GCD both hold an AC record they agree on publisher, start year and issue count on nearly every
# shelf; the AC reprint line's habit of reviving a title after ten years (Blazing Western 1989+2000,
# Durango Kid 1990+2000, Rocky Lane Western 1989+2000) shows up as a GCD span like "1989-2000" that
# matches our two files exactly — that span agreement is the strongest single signal on this batch.
#
# RULE I APPLIED to the trade-only shelves (Not All Robots, The Prism): the shelf's identity is
# whatever the stored shelf-level CV volume describes, and I name the GCD series that describes THE
# SAME THING. Where the per-file GCD link points at a collected-edition series while the CV volume is
# the floppy run, I say so and take the run's GCD row from the dump instead; the collected-edition
# row is the FILE's container record, not the run's identity.
