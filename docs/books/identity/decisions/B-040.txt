# the Image three-record trap in its purest form: a "COL <Title> (<year+1>) (Digital) (DR & Quinch-Empire)"
# trade sitting alone on a shelf, a CV volume of N + a GCD row of N (the RUN), and a same-title count-1
# record a year later in BOTH providers (the TRADE). The stored ComicVine link is the count-1 trade record
# on 12 of the ~55 trade-only shelves here (Jules Verne's Lighthouse, Loose Ends, Lost Vegas, New Masters,
# Nowhere Men, On the Stump, Planetoid, Rain Like Hammers, Rogues' Gallery, Severed, Monkey Meat, Mercy) —
# every one gets wrong-cv-link and the run on the S line, per the trades-vs-run ruling.
# Folder conventions seen: Image\<Title> (<start year>) is the house shape and its year is the RUN's start
# year even when the only file inside is a trade dated a year or two later; "Minis & Extras", "Annuals and
# Specials", "_Trades, Minis and One-shots" and "Deluxe Editions" are bucket subfolders that name no run;
# "_Dexlue Editions" (sic) is the Radiant Black one.
# Systemic: ComicVine is thin on 2015-2023 Image OGNs that GCD covers and vice versa — 14 shelves here get
