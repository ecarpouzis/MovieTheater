# B-026 — 150 shelves and 148 of them are ONE folder: `Fantagraphics`, filed one book per shelf. The tier-B
# shape here is the least ambiguous in the programme so far: ComicVine holds a count-1 volume named exactly
# as the book, published by Fantagraphics in the file's own year, and the Grand Comics Database holds a
# count-1 series with the same name, publisher and year. That is two independent legs on one book, so the
# default line is 0.95 — and because both legs are ITEM-level for a one-book series (CV's single issue id,
# GCD's single gcd_issue row) every book also gets its own `I` line, which is the shelf's real identity.
#
# Findings worth the ledger:
#  (1) **GCD keeps the SUBTITLE our filenames and ComicVine drop.** That is the whole reason 9 of these
#      shelves looked leg-less in the packet. Re-probing on a distinctive fragment found every one:
#      "Beirut Won't Cry" -> 118134 "Beirut Won't Cry- Lebanon's July War: A Visual Diary"; "Giraffes in My
#      Hair" -> 57161 "…: A Rock 'n' Roll Life"; "Not Funny Ha-Ha" -> 91272 "…: A Handbook for Something
#      Hard"; "Invisible Ink" -> 92809 "…(A Graphic Memoir)"; "Mort Gerberg on the Scene" -> 140016
#      "…: A 50-Year Cartoon Chronicle". GCD also adds a CREATOR prefix ComicVine drops: "Casey at the Bat
#      and Other Diamond Tales" is GCD's "Willard Mullin's Casey at the Bat…" (179998). Probe the FRAGMENT,
#      not the title.
#  (2) **The Fantagraphics EC Artists' Library is ONE GCD series (69863, 39 volumes) where ComicVine mints a
#      volume per book** — the Epic Collection shape. Three shelves here are volumes of it (v21 Master Race,
#      v29 Accidents and Old Lace, v36 Kamen's Kalamity). I did NOT put gcd=69863 on the three S lines: that
#      would demand an `F merge-with` and fuse three different books into one shelf, and unlike the Epic
#      Collection case these books are filed separately in `Fantagraphics`, not in one line folder. The S
#      lines take the ComicVine volume at 0.9 and each book's own GCD ISSUE id (with its ISBN) is on the
#      `I` line, which is where the identity actually belongs. Presented, not resolved — the lead may prefer
#      the merge. (PLAN: the same question will recur on every Fantagraphics reprint line.)
#  (3) **Five shelves are a TRADE whose numbered RUN both legs also hold** — the run wins and the stored
#      link was the trade record every time: Delphine (run CV 35963 / GCD 21670, 4 issues), Fuzz & Pluck in
#      Splitsville (46314 / 57163, 5), Inner City Romance (54270 / 17069, 5), Meat Cake (52109 / 19053, 18),
#      Neat Stuff (7244 / 7812, 15). Four of the five carry `F wrong-cv-link`.
#  (4) **GCD indexes PRINTINGS and Fantagraphics reprints a lot**: Abandoned Cars is 56257 (2008 hardcover)
#      + 56258 (2010 softcover, which is the rip we hold); Four Color Fear has three printings under one
#      series; Everybody Is Stupid, Bread & Wine and Disillusioned Illusions each have a second row years
#      later. The S line takes the first edition both legs agree on, the `I` line takes the printing we hold.
#  (5) **A parsed key that is one common word folds a second, unrelated book onto the shelf**: "Mean" pulled
#      in Benjamin Marra's *What We Mean by Yesterday* (2024), "Last Dance" pulled in *Salome's Last Dance*
#      (2023). Both are `F misfiled` with the intruder given its own `I` line.
#  (6) Nine shelves are genuinely absent from GCD (`gcd=-` + `F provider-missing`): the very newest and the
#      very smallest Fantagraphics books. ComicVine indexes all of them.
#
# `I` lines: one per BOOK, not per file — a shelf holding two or three identical rips of one book (Anaïs
# Nin, Beirut, Fran, Ghosts and Ruins, Grosz, Hobo Mom, How to Read Nancy, Messages in a Bottle, Mox Nox,
# Not Funny Ha-Ha, Accidents and Old Lace) gets one line naming every item it covers.
