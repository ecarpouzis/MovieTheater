# A-008 — DC: the #DC Events read-order folders (Convergence, Rebirth, Our Worlds at War, Year of the Villain,
# Countdown, Flashpoint, Final Crisis) and then the alphabetical DC\<Title> shelves from "1st Issue Special" to
# "The Fury of Firestorm". Almost every shelf here is one publishing run whose CV volume and GCD series agree
# on publisher, start year and count, and whose ladder is nailed down by the ripper's own "(of NN)" — the
# solicited length — so most lines are 0.95 on two legs plus that arithmetic.
#
# Three things recur and are the only real reading in this batch:
# 1. The reading-order folders duplicate files. "#DC Events\...\<NNN> <event>" folders hold a second rip of an
#    issue that also lives on the title's own shelf, which is where every ratio 2.00 and every "1 number names
#    more than one file" comes from. Duplicate rips are residue, so those shelves are 0.9, not 0.95.
# 2. GCD's round2-folder method handed FIVE Convergence tie-in shelves the EVENT umbrella series 87901
#    "Convergence" (9 issues = Convergence #0-8) — a textbook hull, and on S17311 it handed Superman's Secret
#    Files the Harley Quinn one-shot's series (11682), which would have MERGED two unrelated shelves. Where the
#    dump names the real per-title series I used it (88142, 88478, 88149, 15228, 114409); where it names none
#    (S4216, S4221) the line carries CV only at 0.9 with provider-missing.
# 3. Trades that are NOT editions of the shelf's run: DC Universe by Alan Moore/by John Byrne (author
#    anthologies), the 2012 "Nuclear Men" trade under the 1982 Firestorm run, five "World of Flashpoint
#    Featuring X" collections over a 3-issue mini, Masamune Shirow's Black Magic under DC's 1973 Black Magic,
#    a 2004 Planet Heist chapter under the 1990 Adam Strange mini, five Countdown Specials under Countdown.
#    Those get F misfiled / split-needed and the shelf is judged on its majority run.
#
# One instrument warning: the Glorith rips' ComicInfo Web ids are NOT GCD issue ids in our dump. I resolved
# 29 of them (S9744's 189906, S17311's 157428, S22010's 149824, S17290's 93952, and the Arion / Blue Devil /
# Chronos / El Diablo / Extreme Justice / Firestorm / DC Universe sets): they either do not exist or land on
# unrelated series (23078 -> House of Mystery #183, 129019 -> Kalle Anka & C:o). No 1.0 was claimed from one.
