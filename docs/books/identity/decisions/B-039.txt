# G.I. Joe block filed under Image. The house shape is the Image three-record trap the ledger names, and
# it fires at the IDW rate: 24 `wrong-cv-link` lines where the stored link is the same-title COUNT-1
# collected record a year after the mini while the run sits below it and GCD's count-N row confirms it.
#
# Findings worth the ledger:
#  (1) **GCD spans a shelf ComicVine splits, three more times.** Bloodstrike is ONE GCD series 11504
#      (Image 1993-2018, 34) covering our ladder 1-33 across four ComicVine volumes (18188/47354/83124/
#      111535); Image+ is one GCD series 103474 (28) over CV's two volumes; Cyberforce Origins is one GCD
#      miniseries 15809 (3) where ComicVine mints a volume per issue (Cyblade/Stryker/Impact). Hinges is
#      the same shape and the reverse of the packet's leg: GCD 230052 (Image 2015-2017, 3) is our three
#      books while the per-file matcher took Tokyopop's German edition.
#  (2) **The Image trade line is a merge, not a rival identity.** Four sibling shelves are one comic split
#      by a subtitle or an edition — Danger Club Death/Rebirth, Image+ v1/v2, Inferno Girl Red's collection
#      and its issues — and take `merge-with` rather than two different links.
#  (3) **A one-book publisher can disagree with itself on the publisher column.** Common Foe is GCD 14294
#      (Image 2005-2006, 4) and CV 59689 (Desperado 2006, 4) for the same four issues; our files are 2005-
#      2006 in an Image folder, so GCD has it right and the shelf is capped at 0.9 with both named.
#  (4) **Six shelves are several comics and take `R` + `split-needed`**: Grrl Scouts (two different runs'
#      trades), Impaler (2006 and 2008 relaunch, both numbered 1-3), Impact Winter (the 2022 one-shot and
#      the 2023 Rook one-shot), G.I.Joe Master & Apprentice (v1 and v2, both #1-4), Invincible Universe
#      Compendium (one Universe Compendium book and one Invincible Compendium Vol. 3) and — capped at 0.9
#      instead, because one file dominates — Bliss and Bolero, each an Image book with a foreign or
#      webcomic namesake folded in from another folder (`misfiled`).
#  (5) **Nine honest `provider-missing` flags, every one probed directly**: Bulletproof Coffin's Thousand
#      Yard Stare, Captain Stoneheart, Carbon Grey Omnibus, Criminal: Special Edition, Cover Girls,
#      First Knife, G-Man: Learning To Fly, Hazed and Dr. Atomic's Pipe & Dope Book are absent from the
#      GCD dump; the G.I. Joe Tim Seeley Sketchbook is a tenth.
#  (6) GCD hides two of these behind a LONGER title — "Griz Grobus and the Tale of Azkon's Heart" (212789)
#      and "Holiday Party (One-Shot)" (194664) — and one behind a shorter one, "Clover Honey" (123618) for
#      ComicVine's "Clover Honey Special Edition". Probe both directions before writing provider-missing.
