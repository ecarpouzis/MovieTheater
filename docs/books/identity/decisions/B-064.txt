# shelf, the Reid Fleming / Scud / Fireman Press cluster, the whole 23-folder Tank Girl tree, the whole
# The Crow tree, the whole The Tick tree, and a long tail of one-file small-press books.
# Conventions of this batch: (1) the Titan/IDW Tank Girl mini shelves are the IDW/Dynamite shape at full
# strength — EVERY mini has a same-title count-1 collected record a year later in BOTH providers, and on
# the seven trade-only Tank Girl shelves the per-file matcher picked that count-1 record, not the run;
# (2) New England Comics' Tick specials are a GCD hole about half the time — GCD indexes the annuals it
# has under "The Tick's ..." with the possessive the CV record drops, and re-probing with the possessive
# recovered four legs the packet missed; (3) the 1970s underground one-shots (Crumb, Shelton) are a clean
# 0.95 pair in both providers except where GCD files the first printing under the cartoonist's own name
# (Feds 'N' Heads 76819 = Gilbert Shelton, 17022 = Print Mint); (4) Bubble Comics, Space Between,
# Antarctic Press digital and Clattertrap are ComicVine-only, as the ledger says; (5) a trade-only shelf
# in a `<Title> (<year>)` folder is the RUN's folder — the run takes the S line and the trade an I line.
