# A-027 — 130 shelves, the middle of Marvel's _X-Men Complete Chronology V2 reading-order folder: entries 0663
# to 0988, i.e. 2005 (Wolverine: Soultaker, House of M) through 2012 (AvX). Nearly every shelf is a mini or a
# one-shot whose issues sit in a numbered chronology folder, with the occasional trade rip filed beside them
# or under the title's own folder elsewhere.
# The batch is unusually clean: 100 of the 130 shelves are a CV volume and a GCD series that agree on
# publisher, year and count against a complete "(of NN)" ladder, and the stored CV link is right on every one
# of them. The interesting work was in three places.
# 1. The Chronology's SECOND rip. `_Marvel Major Event Chronology (digital)` re-files the same books a second
#    time under the event, and the title's own folder a third; that is the whole of every ratio 2.00 here —
#    Dark Reign: The List (Wolverine and X-Men), X Necrosha and The Gathering, all four Chaos War one-shots,
#    Curse of the Mutants Saga and Smoke and Blood, Blind Science, Wolverine: Saudade / Savage / Mr. X,
#    Rampaging Wolverine, Decimation: The Day After. Fifteen shelves, no second run among them.
# 2. Trades filed onto an issue shelf. Where the trade collects the run beside it, it costs nothing and gets
#    an I line on its own collected record; where it collects a DIFFERENT comic it is `F misfiled`. Two of
#    those are the same trap: "COL X-Men Vol. 05 - War Machines" and "COL X-Men Vol. 03 - First to Last" are
#    books of the X-Men (2010) v3 ongoing that the title key pulled onto Civil War: X-Men and First Class
#    Finals. Two more are later relaunches' trades: Dark X-Men - The Mercy Crown (2024) on the 2010 mini.
# 3. NEW CONVENTION (goes in the ledger): Marvel Digital Comics Unlimited exclusives. Five shelves here are
#    8-12pp MDCU webrips (Wolverine: Development Hell, The Dust From Above, Carni-Brawl, the six Astonishing
#    Tales: Wolverine/Punisher chapters, Weapon X: First Class - Gambit in The Job). ComicVine indexes them
#    (often with publisher literally "Marvel Digital Comics Unlimited"); GCD does NOT — its per-file leg
#    answers every one of them with a `round2-folder` copy of a folder neighbour. `gcd=-` + provider-missing
#    is the honest line, and the page count (8-12pp against a 22-40pp print issue) is what identifies them.
#    Astonishing Tales is the sharp case: six 9pp digital chapters and six print issues, both numbered #1-6
#    on one shelf — co-equal runs, so R + split-needed rather than a dominant run with residue.
# `round2-folder` was wrong in all nine places it appears (the Pulse: House of M Special given House of M's
# own series; Wolverine: Firebreak given Deadpool/GLI's; Development Hell and The Dust From Above given a
# French Panini Wolverine; Carni-Brawl given Wolverine: The Best There Is; Messiah War Sourcebook given the
# Cable ongoing; the AvX Program given the AvX mini). The dump named the right per-title row in seven of nine.
# One bad LOCG bridge worth recording: gcd 25582 (Deadpool / GLI - Summer Fun Spectacular) bridges to LOCG
# 109237 "Wolverine: Firebreak" — the bridge is naming a different comic, and it is the same folder neighbour
# the round2-folder leg copied. A support count is not a name check, exactly as the brief says.
# I-line convention as in A-016..A-026: a book gets an I line only when its own record differs from or is
# finer than the S line's; twin count-1 rows in the dump that the packet cannot separate take 0.7.
