# THE FINDING OF THE BATCH, and it should go straight into the ledger: **a Marika-Empire rip's ComicInfo
# `Web` id is a ComicVine ISSUE id.** I checked 21 of them against the ComicVine reference and every single
# one resolves, and every one's volId is exactly the volume the packet's stored link names — 987624 -> 150104,
# 827514 -> 133607, 1041196 -> 156188, 992913 -> 150929, 1070381 -> 159856, and so on down the list. That is
# the 1.0 shape the brief defines: the collection asserts the record and a leg agrees. Twenty-eight shelves in
# this batch take 1.0 on it — the whole _Marvel Facsimile Editions folder plus Iron Man: The Legend and the
# Danny Ketch Omnibus — and each of those books also gets an I line carrying its ComicVine ISSUE id, the first
# item-level CV ids in this program. This is the OPPOSITE of the ledger's Glorith rule (Glorith Web ids are
# not GCD issue ids); the ripper decides, and Marika's ids are gold.
# The Facsimile folder is otherwise a trap factory: the per-file GCD leg gives EVERY one of the twenty-four
# single-book facsimile shelves the same wrong series — 185205 "Moon Knight" 2022-2024 Panini France — by
# round2-folder. Twenty-four for twenty-four wrong, and a foreign-language row at that. The dump names the
# right per-title row for twenty-three of them; X-23 No. 1 has no GCD row at all (gcd=- + provider-missing).
# Trade-only shelves of a run that exists, seven of them, and the stored link is the collected record in six:
# Maestro: Symphony in a Gamma Key (CV 134227, count 1, against the five-issue Maestro 2020), Maestro: War and
# Pax (CV 138155 count 1 against CV 133356's five), Ms. Marvel by Saladin Ahmed (CV 122072, the Vol 01 TPB,
# against the eighteen-issue Magnificent Ms. Marvel), The Punisher: War Machine (CV 111150, count 2 = our two
# books, against the sixteen-issue Punisher 2018), and two where the link is simply a DIFFERENT COMIC:
# S14200's two "Punisher by Greg Rucka" trades are linked to CV 44773 "Wolverine by Greg Rucka Ultimate
# Collection", and S64671's "Fantastic Four Annual #6 Facsimile Edition" is linked to Panini Verlag's GERMAN
# Fantastic Four (CV 142786) when its own record is CV 124632 / GCD 155420.
# The prettiest arithmetic: Luke Cage's two trades are judged #1-5 and legacy #166-170, and 5 + 5 = 10, the
# exact count CV 101484 and GCD 114165 both record for Luke Cage (2017). Iron Fist: The Living Weapon (#1-6 +
# #7-12 = 12), Royals (#1-5 + #6-12 = 12), Ironheart (#1-6 + #7-12 = 12) and Infamous Iron Man (#1-6 + #7-12
# = 12) all close the same way.
# Foreign-language legs are a cluster again: Panini France's Moon Knight on 24 facsimiles, Panini Deutschland
# on Hulk vs. Thor and Tony Stark: Iron Man, Panini UK on the 2025 Fantastic Four. The English pair is in the
# rip and the dump every time.
# Eleven shelves carry a CV volume with no CvVolume row (F needs-fetch) — heavier here than in A-023, and all
# of them are volumes the local rip named.
# Digital-vs-print once more: Marvel's The Avengers Prelude: Fury's Big Week is eight 13pp digital chapters
# (CV 45812, Marvel Digital Comics Unlimited) where GCD indexes only the four-issue print mini — gcd=-.
# I-line convention as in A-016..A-023: a book gets an I line only when its own record differs from or is
# finer than the S line's.
