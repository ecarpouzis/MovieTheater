# B-067 — tier B: the tail of Source Point Press, the Substack/Kickstarter folder, Sumerian, the whole of
# TKO Studios, the whole of Titan Comics, and the first TokyoPop / Top Shelf shelves.
# Conventions of this batch: (1) TKO Studios publishes every book twice at once — a 6-issue floppy run and a
# same-title count-1 collected record, in BOTH providers — and the stored link is the collected record on
# nine of the fifteen TKO shelves here (Eve of Extinction, Goodnight Paradise, Pound for Pound, Redfork,
# Sentient, The Banks, The Fearsome Doctor Fang, The Pull, Djeliya excepted), which is the IDW/BOOM shape at
# full strength; (2) Titan is the same shape with a twist — the run and its trade are both in both legs, and
# on fourteen Titan shelves the stored link is the count-1 trade (Reflections, Cowboy Bebop, ExtraOrdinary,
# Independence Day, Minky ×2, Normandy Gold, Peepland, Quake Champions, The Assignment, The Forever War,
# The Prisoner: Uncertainty Machine, Horizon Zero Dawn ×2); (3) Titan's licensed European albums (Azimut,
# Cutting Edge, Tyler Cross, Yragaël) are the Delcourt/Europe Comics shape — ComicVine mints the English
# volume, GCD holds only the original-language album line, so they pair at 0.9 with the language named;
# (4) Titan's Doctor Who "Year Two" runs exist in GCD (92899, 95387) and NOT in the ComicVine rip, the exact
# mirror of the usual hole; (5) six shelves are several different comics with a record each and no spanning
# record — Doctor Who specials, Dark Souls, Mandrake, Sea of Thieves, Shades of Magic, Torchwood, The
# Michael Moorcock Library, Elric, Conan FCBD and The Cabinet — and take R + split-needed; (6) TKO and the
# small Titan art books are a real GCD hole (Graveneye, Sacred Lamb, Black Mass Rising, The Witches of World
# War II, Girl Rebels, Don Coppola, Jimi Hendrix, Gil Scott-Heron, Sherlock Holmes, A Smart and Courageous
# Child, Underground) — `provider-missing` on the GCD side is the honest answer on 34 shelves here.
