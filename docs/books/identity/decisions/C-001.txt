# C-001 — one tier-C shelf: S37 "2000 AD", 2,332 files, the British weekly 1977-2025 plus nine annuals/specials.
# The conflict that tiered it C: ComicVine keeps ONE volume for the whole weekly while GCD splits it by
# PUBLISHER into four consecutive series (IPC -> Fleetway -> Egmont Fleetway -> Rebellion), and four "issue
# numbers" (2013, 2014, 2015, 2017) that name more than one file are the YEAR on a Christmas bumper prog's
# cover read as a prog number, not a second run. Folders are prog-range buckets (0000-0249 ... 2001-2250),
# not separate comics.
S 37 cv=19752 gcd=11293 0.9 | CV 19752 "2000 AD" 1977 Rebellion count 2483 with cached issues #1-2417 (2319) and yearD 0 is the whole weekly, and the per-file matcher put that one volume on 2332/2332 files; our ladder 0-1243, 1245-1250, 1280, 1350-2417 sits entirely inside that cached span at ratio 2327/2483 = 0.94, and 866 files carry ComicInfo Series "2000 AD" + Publisher "Rebellion". GCD splits the same weekly by publisher into 11289 (IPC 1977-1987, 443), 11295 (Fleetway 1987-1997, 478), 11294 (Egmont Fleetway 1996-2000, 191) and 11293 (Rebellion 2001-?, 1222) — 2334 issues summing to ComicVine's single volume — so the publisher-change rule applies and the majority leg 11293 (1072 of our 2332 files, the widest span and the live one) carries the line; 0.9, not 0.95, because nine files in "Annuals and Specials" are DIFFERENT comics (FCBD offerings, the Sci-Fi/Villains specials, Festive Thrillpower, Mega-City Five-0!, three year-cover bumper progs) and three of those have no record of their own in our data.
F 37 partial-rip | the ladder is 0-1243 then 1245-1250, then a single 1280, then 1350-2417: progs 1251-1349 (99 issues, 2001-2003) and 1244's neighbours 1251+ are simply absent from the rip, and the two-file "2000AD (Unsorted)" folder plus the isolated prog 1280 filed under Misc\Epic - Marshal Law (item 105625) are the leftovers of that partial fill, not a second run.
N 37 GCD's per-file leg reported round2-series on 166 files (the method the ledger marks as unreliable) but all four series it reached are consecutive publisher-eras of the SAME weekly (11289/11293/11294/11295), so for once the method's answer is harmless; num matched 1892 files by prog number inside the name-chosen series.

Wrong-coordinate, not a second rip.
