# D-006 — tier D (no stored provider leg), 120 shelves: the tail of Fantagraphics (the strip-reprint LINES —
# Maakies, Macanudo, Pogo, Peanuts Every Sunday, Popeye Sundays, Crepax, Young Romance — plus the artist
# folders), the whole First Second list, Frank Miller Presents, Glénat scanlations, and the long
# `Graphic Novels` bucket folder.
# Four shapes. (1) The LINE volume against per-book GCD rows, or the reverse: ComicVine keeps ONE "Maakies"
# volume of 6 whose issue names are our six filenames while GCD mints a row per book (and the same folder
# split hits Pogo, Peanuts, Popeye Sundays, Crepax, History Comics) — those shelves are merge chains, and
# the per-book page counts and ISBNs place every volume. (2) The 2022-2025 Fantagraphics / First Second /
# D&Q singleton GCD indexes and the ComicVine rip does not — `cv=- gcd=<row>` + provider-missing. (3) The
# `Graphic Novels` bucket is a folder, not a run: ~55 unrelated one-off books, most of them micro-press,
# self-published, webrip or scanlation, and the packet's candidate block returned the SAME six noise rows
# ("The Discworld Graphic Novels", "Dr. Seuss Graphic Novels", "Graphic Novels as Philosophy") on nearly
# every one of them because the parsed key is the folder name. Every decision here comes from re-probing
# the file's own title; where that finds nothing in either catalog the honest line is R + provider-missing,
# and that is the single commonest verdict in this batch. (4) Scanlations of French albums take the
# original-language row where one exists (the Europe Comics ruling), and an R where it does not.
