# R-006 — a two-shelf REVISIT. S18118 "The Black Moon Chronicles" and S18130 "The Black Moon Chronicles -
# The Prophecy" were both given ComicVine volume 99886 in earlier files with no declared merge, which would
# have fused them silently at the rebuild. Read together they are ONE comic, and the merge is intended.
#
# The evidence is arithmetic, not naming. S18130 holds exactly one file — item 22999, "COL The Black Moon
# Chronicles v13 - The Prophecy (2018) (Europe Comics) (Digital-Empire).cbr", 51pp — and S18118 holds item
# 23376, "The Black Moon Chronicles 13 - The Prophecy (2018) (Europe Comics) (Digital-Empire).cbr", also
# 51pp, from the same ripper in the same year with the same page count. They are two rips of album 13 of one
# line, and the only reason they sit on different shelves is that one filename puts the subtitle after a
# "v13" the parser read as part of the title. Two of S18118's own files do the same thing without escaping
# the shelf ("COL … v08 - The Sword of Justice" beside "08 - The Sword of Justice", and "COL … v20 - A
# Gateway to Hell" beside "20 - A Gateway to Hell"), which is the same duplication pattern and confirms the
# reading: this ripper reissued some albums under a "COL … vNN" name.
#
# The second leg. Europe Comics is the digital English imprint and the Grand Comics Database holds no Europe
# Comics rows at all — probing "Black Moon Chronicles" in the dump returns nothing. Under Eric's ruling the
# original-language row is the right comic in the wrong language: Ledroit and Froideval's line is Dargaud's
# "Chroniques de la lune noire", GCD 89245 (Dargaud 1994-?, 24 albums), whose #13 is "La prophétie" (issue
# 1353377) — the same album number, the same title translated. That pins both shelves to one album of one
# line and settles the question the earlier files left open.
#
