# A-017, in the same proportion: about half the shelves are a complete numbered mini or ongoing whose CV volume
# and GCD series agree on Image, the start year and the count (0.95, no argument), and about half are
# TRADE-ONLY shelves where three records exist for one comic — the RUN (CV volume + GCD dump row, both carrying
# the issue count), a GCD "Collected Series / collected edition" row whose count equals the BOOKS we hold, and
# a CV volume with count 1 named after the trade's subtitle. "Trades vs run" settles every one of them: the S
# line is the RUN, the collected row goes on the books' own I lines.
# Eleven shelves had the stored CV link pointing at the wrong record and needed F wrong-cv-link: Feral, God
# Complex: Dogma, Gogor, Helm Greycastle, Home, Imperial, Injection, Invisible Republic, Kaptara, M.O.M.,
# Primordial, Red City (the count-1 trade record, or in Home's and Primordial's case another publisher's
# collected edition, or in Injection's / Invisible Republic's / Kaptara's case a record whose count equals
# exactly the books we hold).
# The recurring judgement call in this batch is the TRADE LINE THAT OUTLIVES ITS RUN: Hey Kids! Comics!,
# Phonogram, Roche Limit, Pretty Deadly, Man-Eaters, Kaptara, Prism Stalker and October-Faction-shaped shelves
# all hold a Vol. 02 or Vol. 03 that collects a SEQUEL series, not more of the run on the S line. Those are
# capped at 0.9 with the residue named, and every such book still gets its I line on the collected series that
# does cover it.
# What surprised me: three shelves here are genuinely volume-published with no issue run behind them at all —
# Kill Six Billion Demons (a webcomic Image collects as Books), Popgun (a 4-volume anthology) and November /
# Norroway / Pix (OGN volumes) — and for those the collected series IS the identity, at 0.95.
# One shelf refuses: S8258, the two Hack/Slash Deluxe Edition omnibuses, whose reprint line neither leg holds.
