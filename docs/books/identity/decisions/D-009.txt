# D-009 — tier D (no stored provider leg), 120 shelves: the tail of the `Graphic Novels` publisher folders
# (_NBM, _New York Review of Comics, _Pegasus, _Penguin, _RH Graphic, _Saturday AM Presents, _Scholastic &
# Graphix, _Self Made Hero, _Short Box, _Tapas, _Ten Speed Press, _Wonderbound, __Indie Anthologies, moebius
# scanlations), then the whole `Hammer\Halls of Horror` folder, the `Hanna-Barbera & Licenses\Star Trek` tree,
# and `Heavy Metal\Heavy Metal Presents` + `Related_Publications`.
# Three populations with three different answers.
# (1) The trade-publisher graphic novel (NBM, Scholastic, Penguin, RH Graphic, Dark Horse/Tapas): GCD indexes
#     it and the ComicVine rip does not — `cv=- gcd=<row>` + provider-missing at 0.9, 24 shelves; and the
#     2023-2025 books are past BOTH horizons, which is most of the 44 `R` lines here.
# (2) The DC Star Trek tree is the OPPOSITE of tier D's usual shape: everything is indexed twice and the
#     BARCODE settles it. Six shelves carry an EAN-13 that resolves straight to a `gcd_issue` barcode or ISBN
#     (Ashes of Eden 9781563892356 -> 350656, Beginnings 9781563892004 -> 355933, Revisitations -> 350650,
#     the Series Finale -> 264003, Ill Wind -> 5252's four issues, Multiversus -> 215064) — six clean 1.0s,
#     the highest barcode yield of any tier-D batch so far. Its counterweight is the `\Covers` folders: nine
#     shelves that are ONE 1-page cover-index image each ("84Monthly.cbz", "TNGMonthly.cbz", "Annuals.cbz"),
#     which are not comics at all → `R` + not-a-run, the second not-a-run cluster after D-007's Manara folder.
# (3) Heavy Metal's 1977-79 "Heavy Metal Presents" album line is in BOTH catalogs under the album's own title
#     with the "Heavy Metal Presents" only in a cover note (GCD 23271 Candice at Sea, 23273 Conquering Armies,
#     56670 New Tales of the Arabian Nights, 13144 Alien: The Illustrated Story, 2534 Swords of Heaven) — the
#     filename's "Heavy_Metal-19NN-PR-" prefix is what hid every one of them. Its `Related_Publications`
#     sibling folder is the opposite: art books and magazine compilations, half of them in neither catalog.
# One systemic note: the shelf keys in this batch are almost all truncations or ripper tags, so the packet's
# CV-candidate block reached "Hanna-Barbera Yogi Bear" and "Heavy Metal Presents: Arzach" on shelves that have
# nothing to do with either — every line below comes from re-probing the file's own full title or its barcode.
