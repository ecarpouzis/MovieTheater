# Magnetic Press and Mad Cave, the whole `Magazines\MAD Magazine` and `Magazines\Viz` trees, and then 80
# shelves of `Manga\` - which is what this batch really is.
# Five things did the work, and four of them are spellings:
#   1. An ACCENT hides a 26-volume Yen Press line: `lookup.py "Murcielago"` returns one Polish row, while
#      `LIKE '%Murci%'` returns CV 98316 / GCD 118173 "Murciélago" (Yen Press, 23/26) - our twenty volumes.
#      The same class of miss as D-011's slashed O and inner space.
#   2. The MANGA population splits three ways and the split is legible from the file count: an ENGLISH
#      licensed line both legs hold (Viz / Kodansha USA / Seven Seas / Yen Press - 26 shelves, clean 0.95),
#      a SCANLATION of a work with no English edition at all (link the Japanese original at 0.9 with the
#      language named, per the confirmed 2026-09-10 ruling - 8 shelves), and a scanlation of a work NEITHER
#      catalog indexes in ANY language (Akai Yuki, Alexandros, G Senjou Heaven's Door, Waga Na wa Nero,
#      Watashitachi no Shiawase na Jikan, Yuureitou, Exon Depot, Kouishou Rajio, Atomic Love - R, 9 shelves).
#      The packet's `MU / External` line is the tell for the third class: an MU id and nothing else.
#   3. FIVE landed shelves are the other half of a shelf in this batch, and all five were found by grepping
#      the ids before writing: Dragon Ball Super v01-v10 belong to S5805 (v11-v22), the four Viz magazine
#      issues to S21115, Kingdom v08/v17 to S10406, Appleseed v01/v02 to S1153 (v03/v04) and w0rldtr33
#      v01/v02 to S22963. That is the single biggest yield in the batch: ten of its shelves are merges.
#   4. MAD's 2024-2025 DIGITAL SHORTS are a total hole in both catalogs - "MAD - Spy vs Spy" 001-023,
#      "MAD Short Cuts" and the six "Sergio Aragonés Presents a MAD Look at ___" shelves are 5-19pp
#      `digital-mobile` webcomics off madmagazine.com, and eight shelves of this batch are that one thing.
#      The magazine itself is indexed twice over (CV 9318 / GCD 10092 for 1952, CV 109775 / GCD 123822 for
#      the 2018 relaunch, where GCD files modern MAD under "EC" as the ledger already records).
#   5. An ARTBOOK or GUIDEBOOK beside a manga run is its own provider hole: the Fujiko Mine design artbook,
#      the two 3-Gatsu no Lion Review Guidebooks, the MHA Easy Illustration Guide and the Fruits Basket
#      illustration book are all R + provider-missing, while the Attack on Titan Guidebook is in ComicVine
#      alone (CV 76996) - the line between them is whether the book got a Western licensed edition.
