# B-068 — tier B: the whole of Top Shelf (the flat folder, the creator sub-folders, the Johnny Boo minis) and
# the whole of Valiant (modern Valiant Entertainment, the Acclaim years, the _Chronological Valiant reading
# order and the Alien Books revival).
# Conventions of this batch: (1) Top Shelf is an IDW imprint and GCD files EVERY Top Shelf book after 2016
# under publisher "IDW" while ComicVine says "Top Shelf" — the publisher columns disagree by imprint, not by
# comic, and the pair is a clean 0.95 on 40 shelves here; (2) where GCD has no row at all (Hey Mister, Look
# Back and Laugh, Three Rooms in Valerie's Head, Transitions, Bodycount, Cat 'n' Bat, Ward's Valley, Super
# Spy: The Lost Dossiers, Heroes & Monsters, Skull Cat's zero-issue stub) the honest answer is
# `provider-missing`, eleven shelves; (3) Valiant is the count-1-trade shape at full strength — a numbered
# run plus a same-title count-1 collected record in BOTH providers, and the stored link is that collected
# record on 24 shelves here; (4) Valiant's GCD rows often count DOUBLE because GCD indexes the "Pre-Order
# Edition" of every issue as its own row (Doctor Tomorrow 10 for 5, X-O Manowar 16 for 8, Fallen World 10 for
# 5) — a count exactly twice the ComicVine count is that artefact, not a different comic, and it caps those
# lines at 0.9; (5) one folder cut into many shelves by a trade subtitle recurs at Valiant (six Unity trades,
# two Eternal Warrior trades, three X-O Manowar 2017 trades, two Imperium trades) — each set is ONE run and
# takes `merge-with`; (6) the Acclaim one-shots and Yearbooks are indexed cleanly in both legs, and the
# _Chronological Valiant folder is a reading order whose leading number is a position, not an issue number.
