# A-031 — 130 shelves: two stragglers from `_Chronological Valiant`, then the whole Vault Comics folder, the
# whole Vertigo folder, Warren Ellis's Avatar shelves, two Webcomics-and-Strips books and the Wildstorm folder
# (including its Friday the 13th sub-tree and The Authority).
# Vault is a two-shape publisher and the shapes are cleanly separated: single-issue minis of 2-6 issues that
# both legs describe identically (0.95, nothing to say), and trade-only shelves where the STORED CV link is
# the count-1 collected record while both legs also carry the run. Six of those: Hollow Heart (160225 over
# 133874/169620), No One's Rose (132092 over 126030/161059), She Said Destroy (123051 over 119398/146405),
# Interceptor (119854 over the 2016 Heavy Metal run 87166/95425 that Vault only reprinted), and two more in
# the Vertigo half. Vault's own house pattern helps: the dump reliably holds a count-1 same-title row a year
# after each run, which is the TRADE's own record — that is where most of this batch's I lines come from.
# Vertigo is the reverse: nearly every shelf is a complete numbered limited series with two legs agreeing,
# and the trade shelves come with JUDGED RANGES that make the arithmetic exact — Clean Room #1-6+#7-12+#13-18
# = 18 = both legs; Testament #1-4+#5-10+#11-16+#17-22 = 22; Hinterkind 6+6+6 = 18; Art Ops 5+7 = 12;
# Sera & the Royal Stars 5+5 = 10. Where the judged total and a provider count disagree the line is capped
# at 0.9 and the disagreement is named (FBP 24 vs CV's 23, House of Whispers 20 vs CV's 22).
# NEW CONVENTIONS for the ledger: (a) a Wildstorm/Image run that changed publisher mid-stream is SPLIT by GCD
# into two series (DV8: Image 7032 for #1-25, DC 14985 for the last eight) while ComicVine keeps one volume —
# take the majority leg and name the split, exactly like the reprint-library rule; (b) Wildstorm's
# `Friday the 13th` folder mixes Avatar-era and DC-era comics in one directory, so two shelves carry a
# foreign file (`F misfiled`) and the round2-folder leg swapped two Avatar one-shots' series between them;
# (c) `Vertigo\The Nice House by the Sea\Variant Covers` is the DC Black Label 1pp-variant shape again —
# seven 1pp files, three numbers naming more than one file, and not a second run.
# I-line convention as in A-016..A-030: a book gets an I line only when its own record is different from or
# finer than the S line's.
