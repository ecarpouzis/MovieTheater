[L-030] (60 shelf/shelves) sig:collision

[L-151] (60 shelf/shelves) sig:collision sig:withheld

  `cv=-` + `N withheld cv=<id> … S<partner>`, never a merge (S101634 vs S22834); merge-with only for the same comic.
